You might have charges which read similarly. For instance, two charges of possession of child abuse material.

The charges are put on the basis that two devices are found both containing CAM. Two charges are laid. Can we proceed with both charges?

**The short answer:** No, the principle of *nemo debet bis vexari pro una et eadem causa*, translated to "No one ought to be twice harassed for one and the same cause."

Unless there are circumstances that arise from the offending itself that would lead to two possession charges, e.g. police return on a later date, for another search warrant and seize a new device with further CAM on it.
