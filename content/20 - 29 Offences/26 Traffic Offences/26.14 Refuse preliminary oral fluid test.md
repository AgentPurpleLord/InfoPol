>[!INFO] Legislation
>[[Section 49 Offences involving alcohol or other drugs|Section 49(1)(eb) Road Safety Act]] - A *person* is guilty of an offence if he or she — refuses to comply with a requirement made under [[Section 55D Preliminary oral fluid tests|Section 55D]] or [[Section 55E Oral fluid testing and analysis|55E]] when required under that section to do so or refuses to comply with any other requirement made under that section.


# Elements
1. The accused was the alleged offender (identity); and
2. The accused was:
	1. driving or in-charge of a motor vehicle; or
	2. driving a motor vehicle required to stop and remain stopped at a preliminary testing station; or
	3. a person believed by the officer on reasonable grounds to have, within the last preceding three hours, driven or been in charge of a motor vehicle when it was involved in an accident; or
	4. a person believed by the officer on reasonable grounds to have been, within the last preceding three hours, an occupant in a motor vehicle when it was involved in an accident and it has not been established to the satisfaction of the informant which of the occupants was driving or in charge of the motor when it was involved in the accident; and
3. The accused was required to:
	1. if they are inside a motor vehicle, leave the motor vehicle for the purpose of the test; and
	2. undergo a POFT in accordance with [s 55D](https://anzlaw.thomsonreuters.com/Link/Document/FullText?refType=U7&docFamilyGuid=I16458ee8045111e99495db3043f758b0&pubNum=1100190&originationContext=document&transitionType=DocumentItem&docVersion=Law+in+Force&ppcid=3afeb58e823c40a3aafa24448d8feaa0&contextData=\(sc.Category\)); and
4. the requirement was made within three hours of the accused driving, being in charge of or being the occupant of a motor vehicle; and
5. he accused refused to:
	1. leave the motor vehicle; or
	2. comply with the requirement to undergo a POFT.

# Requirements
There are *three* separate requirements:
- A requirement to remain; and/or
- A requirement to accompany; and/or
- A requirement to furnish a sample.

# Defences
## Substantial Character
The defence of "substantial character" is not available for this charge.
