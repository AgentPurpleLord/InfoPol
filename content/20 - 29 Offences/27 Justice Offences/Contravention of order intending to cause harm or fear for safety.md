[[Section 123A Contravention of order intending to cause harm or fear for safety]]

# Mental Harm
- This is defined at s 123A(1) to include psychological harm and suicidal thoughts.
- *Psychological harm* is not defined by the act, but, *psychological abuse* is defined at [[Section 7 Meaning of emotional or psychological abuse]].
- Prosecution **do not** need to prove the accused knew the harm intended or that harm was known to be probable.
- Prosecution **do** need to prove that the accused intended specific harm or knew it was probable and that it falls within the definition of mental harm.
