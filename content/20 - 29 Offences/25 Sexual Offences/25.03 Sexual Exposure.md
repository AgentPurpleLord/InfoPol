---
title: 25.03 Sexual Exposure
---

> [[Section 19 Sexual exposure.md]]
>
> A person (A) commits an offence if; (A) **exposes** (to any extent) (A's) **genitals**; and (A) intends to expose their genistals. The exposure must be **sexual** and is in or within view of a **public place**.

## Example

A park-fiend regularly goes around and exposes his penis to passer-by's in a public park, with the intent for sexual gratification.

## Power of Arrest

[Section 458 of the Crimes Act.](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s458.html "Section 458 of the Crimes Act.")

## Jurisdiction

Summary Offence heard at the Magistrates' Court.

## Defences

**Mistaken** but honest and reasonable belief that the exposure was **not** 'in' or within view of a public place.

## Points of Proof

1. The offence occurred at the place and time alleged.
2. The offender was the accused.
3. The accused intentionally exposed his or her genitals.
4. That exposure was sexual.
5. The exposure was in or within view of a public place.

### Sexual

The exposure must be *sexual* in nature.

Section 19(5) (the offence provision) provides that exposure of the genitals is only sexual where the offender gets sexual gratification or arousal from it. For example, an erect penis is a clear indication of sexual arousal.
