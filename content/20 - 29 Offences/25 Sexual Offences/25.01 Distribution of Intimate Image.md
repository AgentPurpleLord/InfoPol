>[!info]+ [Section 53S Distributing Intimate Image](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s53s.html)
>1. A person (A) commits an offence if—
>	1. (A) **intentionally distributes an image** depicting another person (B); and
>	2. the image is an **intimate image**; and
>	3. **(A) knows** that the image is/probably **is an intimate image**; and
>	4. the distribution of the intimate image is contrary to the community standards of acceptable conduct.

# Example
A person posts a photograph of another person on a social media website without express or implied consent and the photograph depicts a sexual activity.

# Power of Arrest
[Section 459 of the Crimes Act.](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s459.html "Section 459 of the Crimes Act.")

# Jurisdiction
Indictable Offence heard at the Magistrates' Court.

# Defences
Does not apply if B is over 18 **and** has expressly or implied consented to the distribution and manner of distribution.

# Points of Proof
1. Identity
2. Intentionally Distributed Image
3. Distribution Contrary to Community Standards
