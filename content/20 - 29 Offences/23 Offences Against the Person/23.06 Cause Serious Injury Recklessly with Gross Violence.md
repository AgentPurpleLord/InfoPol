>[!info]
>[s. 15B Crimes Act](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s15b.html "Causing Serioues Injury Recklessly in Circumstances of Gross Violence") — A **person** who, **without** lawful excuse, **recklessly** causes **serious injury** to another person in circumstances of **gross violence**.

>[!success]
>The Victorian Judicial College has a [Charge Book section](http://www.judicialcollege.vic.edu.au/eManuals/CCB/index.htm#4932.htm "Charge Book - RCSI Gross Violence") relating to this matter.

# Example
Bob assaults Andy whilst in the company with 2 of his friends. They had planned the assault on Andy and agreed they would carry it out. As a result, Andy was seriously injured and incapacitated.

# Power of Arrest
[Section 459 of the Crimes Act.](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s459.html "Section 459 of the Crimes Act.")

# Jurisdiction
Indictable Offence #iots heard at the County Court.

# Defences
-   Consent
-   Amicable Contest
-   Misadventure or Accident
-   Execution of the Law
-   Lawful Correction or Chastisement
-   Self-Defence

# Points of Proof
1.  Identity
2.  Serious Injury
3.  Causation (did the accused cause the injury)
4.  State of Mind
5.  Circumstances of Gross Violence
6.  Lawful Justification or Excuse
