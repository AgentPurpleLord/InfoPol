# Issue
Where the Magistrates' Court issues a warrant to arrest (or 'bench warrant') for a person that is in custody at the time of it being issued (and therefore has missed court), who can return the warrant unexecuted? Does it need to be police?

# Rule
Magistrates' Court Act 1989:
execution copy, in relation to a warrant, means the copy issued for the purposes of execution;

s 57(8) & (9):
(8) A warrant must be executed by the use of a copy of the warrant, known as the execution copy, or by the use of a copy of the execution copy, including a copy transmitted by facsimile machine or other means of electronic communication. 
(9) The execution copy of a warrant must be in writing and, subject to subsection (1A), must be signed or otherwise authenticated by the person issuing it.
