This will typically come back to [[Section 16 Repeal and re-enactment]]. s 16 can be interpreted to say that when a provision gets revoked and re-enacted, any reference to the old provisions should be construed to be the new provisions, unless Parliament expressly says it shouldn't. 

This type of issue might centre around:

1. A substitution within an Act of a provision for another without any changes to the numbering of the provision, but with modifications to the text of the provision.
2. A substitution within an Act of a provision where changes are made to the number of that provision.
3. A substitution within an Act of a provision where changes are made to both the text and numbering of the provision.
