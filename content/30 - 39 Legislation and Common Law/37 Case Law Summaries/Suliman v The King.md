CRIMINAL LAW – Appeal – Sentence – Plea of guilty to charges of recklessly causing serious injury, affray, and common law assault – Random street violence – Applicant and co- offender assaulted three victims in public place – Applicant under influence of alcohol and cocaine – Applicant struck one victim to the head when victim was in vulnerable and defenceless state, causing him to sustain a severe traumatic brain injury – Several mitigating factors, including applicant’s deprived background and impaired mental functioning – Whether judge erred in application of Bugmy and Verdins principles – Whether unjustifiable disparity with co-offender’s sentence – Whether sentence manifestly excessive – Total effective sentence of 6 years and 6 months’ imprisonment with non-parole period of 4 years and 6 months within range – Leave to appeal refused.

# Overview of Appeal
Ground 1: The learned sentencing judge erred in his application of the Bugmy principles. 
Ground 2: The learned sentencing judge erred in failing to apply Verdins principles 3 and 4. 
Ground 3: The sentences imposed on charges 2 and 3 give rise to a justifiable sense of grievance, when compared with the sentence imposed on the applicant’s co-accused Mr Haddara.
Ground 4: The individual sentences on charges 1–4, orders for cumulation and non-parole are manifestly excessive having regard to all relevant matters, including the applicant’s guilty pleas and disadvantaged background.
**Leave to appeal refused**
# Offending
- 10pm on 29 OCT 2022, accused drove to a bar in Southbank with Mr. HADDARRA. Met up with unknown male.
- Mr VIDOVIC, RAHMANI and SENVARDAR were friends also at the bar that night.
- At 12:30am on 30 OCT 2022, bar closed and patrons exited onto Southbank Promenade.
- RAHMANI walked out of bar, brushed shoulders with unknown male who was with HADDARRA. RAHMANI apologised and continued to walk outside. Unknown male shouted at him, RAHMANI offered to shake his hand. An argument has broken out.
- A physical fight then broke out. The accused and unknown male attacked RAHMANI. RAHMANI was uppercut with a significant degree of force. 
- A female patron tried to break up the fight but got hit in the mouth and knocked to the ground. 
- VIDOVIC got involved and went after HADARRA, he was struck to the back of the head with force. He ended up falling to the ground and his head hit the concrete pavement. 
- VIDOVIC was taken to hospital with life threatening injuries, underwent neurosurgery and sustained a traumatic brain injury. 
- RAHMANI sustained jaw fractures, displaced left lower jawbone and fractures to both sides of his nose requiring specialist surgery and lost a tooth.
- SENVARDAR sustained a broken nose.
- The accused and unknown male were arrested a few days later and both made no comment interviews.
