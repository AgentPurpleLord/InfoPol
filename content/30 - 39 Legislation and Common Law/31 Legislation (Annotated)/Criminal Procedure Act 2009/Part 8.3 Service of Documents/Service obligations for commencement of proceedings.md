
>[!IMPORTANT] Service within 7 days if self-issued charges
>- If you self-issue your charges (without attending the court in person); you must
>- Send the original copy to the relevant court within 7 days of self-issue.
>- You may then send your execution copy at a later time.

A police officer or public official who issues a summons to answer a charge must file the charge-sheet and summons with a registrar of the Magistrates’ Court within 7 days of signing the charge sheet. If he or she fails to file the charge-sheet and summons, the court may strike out the charge.[183](https://jade.io/article/281812)
