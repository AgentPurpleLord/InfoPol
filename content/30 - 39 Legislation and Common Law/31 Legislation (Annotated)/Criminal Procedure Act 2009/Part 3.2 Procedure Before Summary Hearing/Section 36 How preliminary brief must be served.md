>1. A preliminary brief must be served personally on the accused in accordance with [[Section 391 - Personal service]] unless the informant is satisfied that ordinary service is appropriate in all the circumstances.
>2. In considering whether to effect service of a preliminary brief by ordinary service, the informant must consider whether it is an appropriate method of service in all the circumstances as known by the informant including—
>	1. the nature and gravity of the alleged offence;
>	2. whether the accused has previously been found guilty or convicted of any similar offence;
>	3. the period of time that has elapsed since the accused's address for service was ascertained;
>	4. the manner of service of the summons to answer to the charge.
