> 1. The court may specify in a final order the period for which the order is in force.
> 2. In making a decision as to the period for which the final order is to be in force, the court *must* take into account—
> 	1. that the *safety of the protected person* is paramount; and
> 	2. any *assessment by the applicant* of the *level and duration of the risk* from the respondent; and
> 	3. if the applicant is not the protected person, the protected person's views, including the protected person's assessment of the level and duration of the risk from the respondent.
> 3. The court may also take into account any matters raised by the respondent that are relevant to the duration of the order.
