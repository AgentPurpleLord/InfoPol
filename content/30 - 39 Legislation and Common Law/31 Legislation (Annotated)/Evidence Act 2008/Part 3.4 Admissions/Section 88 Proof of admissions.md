> For the purpose of determining whether evidence of an admission is admissible, the court is to find that a particular person made the admission if it is reasonably open to find that he or she made the admission.

- That a particular person made the admission; and
- A finding under this section is only made for the purpose of determining whether the evidence is admissible; and
- applies to the question of whether an alleged statement is an admission at the admissibility stage.
