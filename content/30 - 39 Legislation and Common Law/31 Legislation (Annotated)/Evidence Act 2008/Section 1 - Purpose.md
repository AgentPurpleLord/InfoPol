>[!QUOTE] Purpose
>The purpose of this Act is to make fresh provision for the law of evidence that is uniform with Commonwealth and New South Wales law.[^1]

- Establish new rules & regulations around Evidence.
- Rules are to be consistent and in harmony with laws.

# Explanatory Memorandum
[Located on Austlii](http://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/bill_em/eb200890/eb200890.html)
- The Evidence Bill 2008 introduced the Evidence Act 2008.
- It was the first of two bills to introduce uniform and consistent rules for evidence in Victoria.
	- The second bill repealed parts of the Evidence Act 1958 (Vic) and made other framework changes.
- The Australian Law Reform Commission (ALRC) conducted an inquiry in the 1980s that lead the Evidence Act's inception.
- The Evidence Act 2008 was based on the Model Uniform Evidence Bill. The focus was:
	- **Admissibility** of relevant and reliable evidence.
	- Ensure the **probative value** of evidence.
	- Guard against **prejudice** and **unfairness** in court.
