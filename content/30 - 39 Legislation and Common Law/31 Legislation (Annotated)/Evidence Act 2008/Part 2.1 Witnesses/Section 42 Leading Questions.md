>(1) A party may put a leading question to a witness in cross-examination unless the court disallows the question or directs the witness not to answer it.
>(2) Without limiting the matters that the court may take into account in deciding whether to disallow the question or give such a direction, it is to take into account the extent to which—
>	(a) evidence that witness gave in **examination-in-chief**; and
>	(b) witness has interest **consistent** with cross-examiner; and
>	(c) witness **sympathetic** to party conducting cross-examination; and
>	(d) witnesses age, mental/physical/intellectual disability may affect answers.
>(3) Court is to **disallow** question or direct witness not to answer.
>(4) Section does not limit courts power to control leading questions.
