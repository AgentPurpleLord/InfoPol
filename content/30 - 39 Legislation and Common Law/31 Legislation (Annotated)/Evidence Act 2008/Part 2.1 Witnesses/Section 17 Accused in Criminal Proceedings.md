>1. Applies **only** in criminal proceedings.
>2. Accused **not** **competent** to give evidence as **witness** for **prosecution**.
>3. Associated accused **not** **compellable** to give evidence for/against accused, unless associated accused being tried separately from accused.
>4. If a witness is an associated accused being tried jointly with accused in proceeding, the court is to satisfy itself that witness is aware of the effect of (3).

>[!ABSTRACT]- Section 17 - Simplified
>- The accused **cannot** be called as a witness to give evidence by the Prosecution.
>- The accused **can** still give evidence if the Defence call the accused as a witness.
>- If a co-accused is being deal with separately from the accused about the same matter, they **can** be called by the prosecution as a witness.
