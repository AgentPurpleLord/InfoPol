>Except as otherwise provided by this Act—
>(a) every person is **[[Competence and compellability|comptent]]** to give evidence; and
>(b) a person who is competent to give evidence about a fact is **compellable** to give that evidence.



>[!ABSTRACT]+ Section 12 - Simplified
>- Every person is assumed to have ability/knowledge to give evidence in court.
>- Whomever is competent can be made to come to court to give their evidence.

Section 12 is considered an *inclusionary* rule, meaning we make a positive assumption (that everyone is competent and therefore compellable to give evidence).
