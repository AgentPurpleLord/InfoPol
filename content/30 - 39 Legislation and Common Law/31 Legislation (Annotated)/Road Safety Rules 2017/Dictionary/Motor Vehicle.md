>"motor vehicle" means a [[30 - 39 Legislation and Common Law/31 Legislation (Annotated)/Road Safety Rules 2017/Dictionary/Vehicle]] that is used or intended to be used on a highway and that is built to be propelled by a motor that forms part of the [[30 - 39 Legislation and Common Law/31 Legislation (Annotated)/Road Safety Rules 2017/Dictionary/Vehicle]] but **does not include**—
>1. a [[30 - 39 Legislation and Common Law/31 Legislation (Annotated)/Road Safety Rules 2017/Dictionary/Vehicle]] intended to be used on a railway or tramway; or
>2. a motorised wheel-chair capable of a speed of not more than 10 kilometres per hour which is used solely for the conveyance of an injured or disabled person; or
>3. a vehicle that is not a motor vehicle by virtue of a declaration under subsection (2)(b);
