> FAMILY VIOLENCE PROTECTION ACT 2008 - SECT 7
> **Meaning of emotional or psychological abuse**
> For the purposes of this Act, **_emotional or psychological abuse_** means behaviour by a person towards another person that torments, intimidates, harasses or is offensive to the other person.

- Emotional Abuse
	- Threatening to withhold medication
	- Threatening to commit suicide/self-harm to torment family
	- Blaming the victim for all relationship problems
	- Constantly comparing victim with others (undermining self-esteem/worth)
	- Sporadic sulking
	- Withdrawing all interest/engagement (weeks of silence)
	- Emotional blackmail
- Verbal Abuse
	- Swearing and continuous humiliation
	- Attacks focusing on victims intelligence, sexuality, body image and capacity as a parent or spouse
- Social Abuse
	- Systematic isolation from family/friends through rudeness/alienation to them
	- Instigating/controlling move of house to location where victim has no social circle/employment opportunities
	- Forbidding/preventing victim from going out and meeting people
- Psychological Abuse
	- Driving dangerously
	- Destroying property
	- Abusing pets in front of family
	- Making threats regarding custody of children
	- Asserting that police/justice system won't assist, support or believe victim
- Spiritual Abuse
	- Denying/misusing religious beliefs to force victims into subordinate roles
	- Misusing religious/spiritual traditions to justify physical violence/abuse
