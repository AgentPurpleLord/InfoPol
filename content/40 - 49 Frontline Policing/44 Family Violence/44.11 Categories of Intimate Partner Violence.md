[The Kelly Johnson Typology of Intimate Partner Violence](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1744-1617.2008.00215.x) provides four categories.
**Coercive Controlling Violence:** Pattern of emotionally abusive intimidation, coercion and control coupled with physical violence towards their partner. This is done to maintain power and control.
**Violent Resistance:** Occurs where person is in a relationship with violent/coercive controlling partner and responds with violence. E.g. self-defence by the AFM.
**Situational Couple Violence:** Where an argument escalates between partners into violence. This type of violence is not part of wider pattern. Usually arises out of poor conflict/anger management skills.
**Separation-Instigated Violence:** Partners separated with no prior history of violence. Occurs when partner is shocked at being left, then suddenly lashes out at a partner. 

>[!WARNING] Caution Advised
>Do not allow the categories to draw away from the facts of a particular case and more subtle emotional abuse.
