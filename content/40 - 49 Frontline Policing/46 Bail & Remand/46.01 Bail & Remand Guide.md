This guide provides a step-by-step approach to the process. My recommendation for each application is to take the time, write out the steps of this guide and ensure you're making the right decision.
>[!NOTE] When In Doubt: Flow Charts
>[Section 3D of the Bail Act 1977](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/s3d.html) has the legislated pathways of how bail applications are supposed to work. Use them.

# Guide Structure
1. [[Part 1 - Today's Incident]]
	- [ ] Identify today's charges (what you arrested for)
		- [ ] Identify [Sch. 1](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch1.html), [Sch. 2](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch2.html) & [Sch. 3](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch3.html) charges across today's incident
	- [ ] Review your evidence & ensure points of proof are covered ([[20 Offences]])
	- [ ] Compile your reports as required
2. [[Part 2 - Outstanding Matters]]
	- [ ] Review the accused's criminal priors
		- [ ] Identify violent offence convictions within the last 10 years
		- [ ] Identify any offences similar to today's charges
			- [ ] Note down the outcome of the court matter & the date of the offence
	- [ ] Intent to Summons matters?
	- [ ] Bail matters?
	- [ ] Outstanding Warrant Matters?
	- [ ] Community Corrections Orders?
	- [ ] Identify [Sch. 1](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch1.html), [Sch. 2](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch2.html) & [Sch. 3](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch3.html) charges across outstanding matters
	- [ ] Identify relevant Family Violence History (e.g. same victim for today's matters)
3. [[Part 3 - The Accused]]
	- [ ] Identify outstanding matters & charges contained within
		- [ ] Identify Sch. 1, Sch. 2 or Sch. 3 charges across outstanding matters
	- [ ] Is your accused a [Child, Vulnerable Adult or ATSI](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/s3.html)?
		- [ ] Have you notified any required services due to this?
	- [ ] Ask the accused the [[Part 3 - The Accused#^553b13|Personal Circumstances]] questions
4. [[Part 4 - Bail and Remand Applications (Briefs)]]
	- [ ] Prepare the Remand / Bail Application (VP1372) ([[Part 4 - Bail and Remand Applications (Briefs)#^ec51cc|Contents of App]])
	- [ ] Prepare charges & sign all
	- [ ] Complete Bail Revocation Forms as required
6. List Matter at Court (Refer to PFSU Remand HUB)
	1. During Court Hours -> Local Registrar + VLA + Prosecutor
	2. After Court Hours -> BARC Registrar + BARC VLA + BARC Prosecutors
	3. Nominal Informant Organised
7. Post-Remand
	1. Fax CDEB Charge (sub-incident # on each charge) to ensure recording on LEAP, you do not need any other forms faxed off to have it linked to the incident.
