# Outstanding Matters
>[!info]- Complex but doable
>This step often confuses members. Take your time, write down relevant matters on a piece of paper (the same one with your charges). Highlight Sch. 1, 2 or 3 charges.

## Important Points
- Make note of Schedule 1, Schedule 2 or Schedule 3 charges as you go.
- Family violence related matters may place the accused in Schedule 2 if;
	- [Sch. 2 Item 7](http://www6.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch2.html) - **Make Threats to Kill** that is also family violence offence. The only way to check for this is to read the incident narrative.
	- [Sch. 2 - Item 18](http://www6.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch2.html) - **Contravention of IVO / FVSN offences** if the accused in the last 10 years was **convicted** or **found guilty** (struck out does not apply) of an offence which the accused used or threatened violence against **any person**. E.g. an assault with conviction finding is violence against any person.
	- [Sch. 2 - Item 18](http://www6.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch2.html) (b) also includes if the BDM is satisfied that the accused used or threatened violence against the protected person on the IVO / FVSN they breached if they **have not** been found guilty or charged with an offence in connection with using or threatening violence.

## Summons Matters
- Involvements -> Offender -> Select Each Entry and review which were summons matters.
	- An Intent to Summons (ITS) involvement is converted into an Offender involvement when the charges are authorised. Do not rely on the ITS involvements.
- Check [EFAS (magistratesvic.com.au)](https://dailylists.magistratesvic.com.au/EFAS/CaseSearch) , by click 'Criminal List' and entering the accused's surname. Click search. You will see all matters listed at court, including their case numbers.
- **Pending authorisation charges are not considered**, the matter must be listed at court to be considered.

## Bail Matters
- Involvements -> Bail -> Active (ACT) involvements
	- Considering checking each entries History (H), as bail which has been extended for 2+ years is important to make note of on the brief.
- For each count of bail, make note of:
	- Court Case Number
	- Bailed from date and location
	- Bailed to date and location
	- Charges
	- Conditions
	- Informant Registered Number / Last Name

## Warrant Matters
- Involvements -> Warrants -> Active
	- Review the charges and disposition on the accused's warrant.
	- If they have **Failed to answer bail**, you (the person arresting the accused) should be charging them with Fail to Answer Bail.

## Community Corrections Orders or Parole Orders (CCO)
- Criminal History -> Select Court Date with CCO result -> Check Result Section for full narrative
- Police cannot charge with contraventions of a CCO, only Department of Justice may do so.
- The start date and length of the CCO will be stipulated in the result, ensure that the CCO is still in effect.

## Criminal Priors
You only need to include **relevant** criminal priors from the last 12 months. Relevant priors would be:
- Any similar charges to those being laid today or those discovered in the outstanding matters.
- Section 30A (Contravene Bail) and Section 30B (Commit Indictable on Bail) have been repealed, they are no longer applicable.
- Section 30 (Fail to Answer Bail) has **not** been repealed. They are relevant.
