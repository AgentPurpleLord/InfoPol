# Power to Require PBT
>[!INFO] Person found driving or in-charge

“I observed you driving a motor vehicle..." 
OR 
“I observed you in charge of a motor vehicle..."

"I now require you to undergo a preliminary breath test on a prescribed 
device to my satisfaction.” 

## BORG Involved In Accident Within 3 Hours
“I believe on reasonable grounds you were the driver of" 
OR 
“I believe on reasonable grounds you were in charge of"
OR 
(if driver not established):- 
“I believe on reasonable grounds you were an occupant of.."

AND CONTINUE WITH:
"...a motor vehicle involved in an accident within the last 3 hours. I now 
require you to undergo a preliminary breath test on a prescribed device to my satisfaction"
