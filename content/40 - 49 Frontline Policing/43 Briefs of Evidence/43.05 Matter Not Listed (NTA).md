# Execution Charges Failed to Reach Court
If you have sent off original charges, but failed to get the matter listed via a summon (e.g. sending the execution copies) then you can do the following:

1. Fill out a Notice to the Accused (VP 416).
2. Get a new court date and use that as the future court date for the matter.
3. Serve that on the accused.
4. Send the NTA to the court with a copy of your charges to refer to (original or execution).
