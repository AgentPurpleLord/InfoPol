## Must keep up with the law

Copeland v SMith [2000] 1 WLR 1371, Brooke LJ said at [462]:

> [I]t is quite essential for advocates who hold themselves out as competent to practise in a particular field to bring and keep themselves up to date with recent authority in their field. By “recent authority” I am not necessarily referring to authority which is only to be found in specialist reports, but authority which has been reported in the general law reports.


## Court Etiquette

PW Young J wrote "Court Etiquette" (2002) 76 ALG 303-308:

> **Courtesty to opponent**
>
> Rule 1: Always inform your opponent in due time if you are going to be late.
>
> Rule 2: Do not sledge.
>
> Rule 3: There is no duty to help fools.
>
> Rule 4: Respect the procedure.
>
> Rule 5: Do not mention more than two matters at a time.
>
> Rule 6: Respect seniority.
>
> Rule 7: Be courteous in correspondence.
