Section 4 of the Family Violence Protection Act 2008 provides an exhaustive list of definitions. The below are some of the relevant ones:

## Family Violence
[[30 - 39 Legislation and Common Law/31 Legislation (Annotated)/Family Violence Protection Act 2008/Part 1 - Preliminary/Section 1 - Purpose|Section 1 - Purpose]] provides:
- Maximise **safety** for children/adults who experienced FV; and
- Prevent and **reduce FV** to greatest extent possible; and
- Promote **accountability** of perpetrators of FV.

Further definitions of the meaning of *family violence* can be found in Section 5 to 10 of the Family Violence Protection Act 2008.

## Party
These are all considered to be *separate* parties.
- An applicant (e.g. the police); and
- An affected family member (AFM); and
- A respondent (RESP)

## Mention Date
- A first mention date; or
- Another listing date other than contested hearing/appeal.

# Directions Hearing
Parties submit their evidence via Further & Better Particulars. The Magistrate considers that evidence and then provides a 'direction' that they believe the proceedings would go in were they to proceed to a contested hearing. It is designed to resolve the matter early.
