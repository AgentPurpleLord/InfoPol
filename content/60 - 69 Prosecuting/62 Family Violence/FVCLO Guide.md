## FVSN
1. FVSN + AFM Supportive = Finalise 12 months on conditions sought
2. FVSN + AFM Unsupportive = Finalise Safe Contact 12 months; **unless**
	1. Serious allegations; or
	2. Children present during incident and DFFH/CP needed; then
	3. Consider **Full No Contact** or Cond 1, 6 & 7 with adjourn for R/A.
