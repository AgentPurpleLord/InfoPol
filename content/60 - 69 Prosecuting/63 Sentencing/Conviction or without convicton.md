[[Section 8 Conviction or non-conviction]] of the Sentencing Act 1991 is the legislative provision which provides what the court must consider when determining whether to find the accused guilty with or without conviction:
- The *nature of the offence*; and
- The *character* and *past history of the offender*; and
- The *impact* of recording a conviction on the offender's *economic* or *social well-being*; or on his *employment prospects*.
