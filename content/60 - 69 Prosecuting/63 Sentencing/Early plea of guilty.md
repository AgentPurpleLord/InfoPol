The timing of an early plea of guilty matters. The most standard situation is where a plea of guilty is entered at mention or contest mention stage. Arguably, contest mention stage should not attract as much of a discount as mention stage.

A contested hearing certainly lowers the discount granted. 

The circumstances where a guilty plea is *not late*:
1. A plea made to a lesser offence when the prosecution withdraws a higher charge; or
2. Where the prosecution withdraws charges where the accused was reasonably avoiding forensic delay; or
3. If the offender pleads guilty as soon as they are told that it is the only charge they are facing; or
4. The offender pleads guilty as soon as a charge is amended.

In any other circumstances, a plea of guilty is not considered to be early.
