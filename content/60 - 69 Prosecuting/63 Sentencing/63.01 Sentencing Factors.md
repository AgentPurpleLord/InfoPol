Sentencing Factors are found in [[Section 5 Sentencing Guidelines]].

# Factors

| Section   | Wording                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (2)(a)    | the *maximum penalty* prescribed for the offence; and                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| (2)(ab)   | the *standard sentence*, if any, for the offence; and                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| (2)(b)    | *current sentencing practices*; and                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| (2)(c)    | the *nature* and *gravity* of the offence; and                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| (2)(d)    | the offender's *culpability* and degree of responsibility for the offence; and                                                                                                                                                                    |
| (2)(daaa) | whether the offence was motivated (wholly or partly) by *hatred* for or *prejudice* against a group of people with common characteristics with which the victim was associated or with which the offender believed the victim was associated; and |
| (2)(daa)  | the *impact* of the offence on any *victim* of the offence; and                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| (2)(da)   | the *personal circumstances of any victim* of the offence; and                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| (2)(db)   | any *injury*, *loss* or *damage* resulting directly from the offence; and                                                                                                                                                                         |
| (2)(e)    | whether the offender pleaded *guilty* to the offence and if so, the stage in the proceedings at which the offender did so or indicated an intention to do so; and                                                                                 |
| (2)(f)    | the offender's *previous character*; and                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| (2)(g)    | the presence of any *aggravating* or *mitigating* *factors* concerning the offender or of any other relevant circumstances.                                                                                                                       |
