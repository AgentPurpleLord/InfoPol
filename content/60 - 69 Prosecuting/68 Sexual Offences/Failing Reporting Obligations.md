The most common offence you will see on a brief is **failing to comply with reporting obligations**.

These can only be **non-authorised** by an Inspector or above.


## Proof of failing to comply with reporting obligations

[Section 73 of the SORA](https://classic.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/sora2004292/s73.html) e.g. a certificate that without evidence to the contrary, is considered proof.
