> [!INFO] What is it?
> An *application to be deemed not to be a prohibited person* is self-explanatory. A person who *is* a prohibited person, is making an application to have that status removed so that they can own firearms again.
> 
> The application is governed by [[Section 189 - Firearms Act 1996]].

# Family Violence Context
The most common *prohibited person* is someone who has had:
- An intervention order issued **including** a condition for them to have their firearms licence cancelled or revoked; or
- Any person who has had a *final* intervention order (FV/PSIVO) issued against them.
