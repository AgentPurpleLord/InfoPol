# Summary
The High Court of Australia (HCA) has considered the issue of whether the prosecution was obliged to tender the record of interview of an accused when it contained “mixed statements” (that is, both inculpatory comments (admissions) and exculpatory comments). The HCA examined the extent to which this decision falls under ‘prosecutorial discretion’ and whether the prosecution’s obligation to put their case fully and fairly requires the tender of such evidence.

# Impact on Prosecutions
Prosecutors are encouraged to read the full High Court decision and comments therein that relate to the appropriate exercise of prosecutorial discretion. 
Prosecutors should be mindful that while a *Magistrate cannot compel the tender of records of interview* (or any evidence, more broadly), *a decision not to tender such evidence may result in a miscarriage of justice being identified upon review*.
