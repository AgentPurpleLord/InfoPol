1. Identify the outcome of the case; and
2. Locate the reason for that outcome.
	1. Search for markers such as:
		1. *'in my opinion'*; or
		2. *'in my view'*

**Note:** Judges might provide a general definition of the law that is then applied to the question on appeal.

A headnote might provide an *indication of the ratio* but you must identify where it comes from as we cannot refer the court to the headnote. 

The best approach is to ask: **What question is the court required to answer?**
