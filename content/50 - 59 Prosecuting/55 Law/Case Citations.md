# Talking about a case
- Where "R" exists, use "The King" or "The Crown."
- Where "Re" exists, use "In the matter of."
- You don't need to say "and Anor" or "and Ors."

"Jaensch against Coffey, reported in 1984 at volume 155 of the Commonwealth Law Reports, at page 221." (if it is in a report)

"Jaensch against Coffey, 1984, High Court of Australia, case number 52" (if it is square brackets)
