# Overview of Legislation
- [[Section 24 Application for a family violence safety notice| s. 24]]: Power of MOPF to apply to SGT/above for a FVSN.
	- s. 24(a): RESP must be adult for FVSN.
	- s. 24(b): RESP must not be cognitively impaired.
	- s. 24(d): Must be no FVIVO in place between parties.
	- s. 24(e): MOPF BORG until FVIVO made, FVSN necessary to ensure safety AFM, protect property AFM, protect child subjected to FV.
- s. 25: App for FVSN made in person, by fax, telephone or other electronic means.
- s. 26: SGT/above may issue FVSN on same grounds as within s. 24.
