>1. The Magistrates' Court may hear and determine summarily a charge for an offence to which Section 28(1) applies if—
>	1. the **court considers that the charge is appropriate** to be determined summarily, having regard to the matters in subsection (2); *and*
>	2. the **accused consents** to a summary hearing.
>2. For the purposes of (1)(a), the Magistrates' Court must have regard to—
>	1. the **seriousness** of the offence; *and*
>		1. the **nature** of the offence; *and*
>		2. the **manner in which** the offence is alleged to have been **committed**, the apparent degree of organisation and the presence of aggravating circumstances; *and*
>		3. whether the offence forms part of a **series of offences** being alleged against the accused; *and*
>		4. the **complexity** of the proceeding for determining the charge; *and*
>	2. the **adequacy of sentences** available to the court, having regard to the criminal record of the accused; *and*
>	3. whether a **co-accused** is charged with the same offence; *and*
>	4. **any other matter** the court considers relevant.
