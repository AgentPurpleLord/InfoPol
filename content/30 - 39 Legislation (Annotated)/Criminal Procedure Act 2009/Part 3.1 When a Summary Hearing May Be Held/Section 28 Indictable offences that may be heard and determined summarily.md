>1. A charge for any of the following indictable offences may be heard and determined summarily by the Magistrates' Court, if section 29 is satisfied—
>	1. an offence referred to in Schedule 2;
>	2. an indictable offence under an Act / subordinate instrument / offence at common law if the offence is described as being—
>		1. a level 5 or level 6 offence; or
>		2. punishable by level 5 or level 6 imprisonment or fine or both; or
>		3. punishable by a term of imprisonment not exceeding 10 years or a fine not exceeding 1200 penalty units or both—
>
>unless the contrary intention appears in this or any other Act / subordinate instrument.
