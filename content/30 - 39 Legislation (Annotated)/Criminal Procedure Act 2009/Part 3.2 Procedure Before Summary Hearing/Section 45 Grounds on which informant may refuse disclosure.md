>1. The informant may refuse to disclose any information, document or thing that is required by this Division to be disclosed to the accused if the informant considered that disclosure would, or would be reasonably likely to—
>	1. prejudice investigation; or 
>	2. prejudice the fair hearing of the charge / impartial adjudication of case; or
>	3. disclose or enable person to ascertain identity of confidential sources; or
>	4. disclose methods or procedures of investigatory methods; or
>	5. endanger lives / physical safety for person engaged in law enforcement; or
>	6. endanger lives / physical safety of witnesses / family members; or
>	7. result in disclosure of CAM to **accused personally**.

>[!ABSTRACT]- Subclause 1
>- An informant can refuse to give items over to the accused / defence in certain cases.
>- Prejudice is defined as "harm or injury that results or may result from some action or judgment" or "a preconceived opinion that is not based on reason or actual experience."
