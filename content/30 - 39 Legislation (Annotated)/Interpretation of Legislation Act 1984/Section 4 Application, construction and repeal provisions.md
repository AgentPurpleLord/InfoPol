>(1) The provisions of this Act— 
>	(a) unless a contrary intention appears in this Act or in the Act or subordinate instrument concerned, extend and apply to all Acts, whether passed before or after the commencement of this Act, and to all subordinate instruments, whether made before or after that commencement; and 
>	(b) apply to the interpretation of this Act. 
>(2) Nothing in this Act excludes the application to an Act or subordinate instrument of a rule of construction applicable thereto and not inconsistent with this Act. 
>(3) The provisions of this Act which are expressed to apply to Acts passed or subordinate instruments made on or after the commencement of this Act shall not affect the construction of any Act passed or subordinate instrument made before that commencement although that Act or subordinate instrument is continued in force or amended by an Act passed or subordinate instrument made on or after that commencement. 
>(4) The Acts mentioned in the Schedule to the extent to which they are in the Schedule expressed to be amended or repealed are hereby amended or repealed accordingly.


# Explanatory Memorandum
>Clause 4 contains application, construction and repeal provisions. 
>Sub-clause (1) provides that this Act applies to all Acts, whenever passed, and to all subordinate instruments, whenever made, unless a contrary intention appears in this Act or in the Act or subordinate instrument concerned. For the avoidance of doubt this sub-clause also makes it clear that the provisions of this Act apply to the interpretation of this Act. 
>Sub-clause (2) states that any rule of construction unaffected by this Act will continue to operate with respect to Acts and subordinate instruments. 
>Sub-clause (3) provides that the provisions of this Act which are expressed to apply only to Acts passed or subordinate instruments made on or after the commencement of this Act will not apply to Acts passed or subordinate instruments made before that commencement simply because they have been amended or contained in force by Acts . passed or subordinate instruments made on or after that commencement. 
>Sub-clause (4) states that the Acts mentioned in the Schedule are amended or repealed to the extent specified in the Schedule.
