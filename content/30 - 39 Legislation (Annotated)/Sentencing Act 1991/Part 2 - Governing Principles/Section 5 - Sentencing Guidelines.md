>1. The only purposes f or which sentences may be imposed are:
>	1. to punish the offender to an extent and in a manner which is just in all of the circumstances; or
>	2. to deter the offender or other persons from committing offences of the same or similar character; or
>	3. to establish conditions within which it is considered by the court that the rehabilitation of the offender may be facilitated; or
>	4. to manifest the denunciation by the court of the type of conduct in which the offender engaged; or
>	5. to protect the community from the offender; or
>	6. a combination of two or more of those purposes.

**(2AA)**: Provides two points (a) & (b) that in sentencing an offender, the court **must not** have regard to:
- Possibility or likelihood of the length of time spent in custody by the offender will affected by executive action of any kind; or
- Any sentencing practices arising at any time out of section 10 of this Act as in force at any time before its expiry on 22 April 1997.

**(2AB)**: Provides that the court may impose a **less severe** sentence if the offender has assisted, **after sentencing**, law enforcement authorities in their investigation or prosecution of an offence.
