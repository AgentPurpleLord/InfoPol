>The purposes of this Act are—
>1. to promote consistency of approach in sentencing offenders;
>2. to have within the one Act all general provisions dealing with powers of courts to sentence offenders;
>3. to provide fair procedures—
>	1. for imposing sentences; and
>	2. for dealing with offenders who breach or contravene the terms or conditions of their sentences;
>4. to prevent crime and promote respect for the law by—
>	1. providing for sentences that are intended to deter the offender or other persons from committing offences of the same or similar character; and
>	2. providing for sentences that facilitate the rehabilitation of offenders; and
>	3. providing for sentences that allow the court to denounce the type of conduct in which the offender engaged; and
>	4. ensuring that offenders are only punished to the extent justified by—
>5. the nature and gravity of their offences; and
>6. their culpability and degree of responsibility for their offences; and
>7. the presence of any aggravating or mitigating factor concerning the offender and of any other relevant circumstances; and
>	1. promoting public understanding of sentencing practices and procedures;
>8. to provide sentencing principles to be applied by courts in sentencing offenders;
>9. to provide for the sentencing of special categories of offender;
>10. to set out the objectives of various sentencing and other orders;
>	1. to ensure that victims of crime receive adequate compensation and restitution;
>11. to provide a framework for the setting of maximum penalties;
>12. to vary the penalties that may be imposed in respect of offences under the Crimes Act 1958 ;
>13. generally to reform the sentencing laws of Victoria.
