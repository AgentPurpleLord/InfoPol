>1. Circumstances in which a person does not consent to an act include, but are not limited to, the following—
>	1. the person does not say or do anything to indicate consent to the act;
>	2. the person submits to the act because of force, a fear of force, harm of any type or a fear of harm of any type, whether to that person or someone else or to an animal, regardless of—
>		1. when the force, harm or conduct giving rise to the fear occurs; and
>		2. whether it is, or is a result of, a single incident or is part of an ongoing pattern;
>	3. the person submits to the act because of coercion or intimidation—
>		1. regardless of when the coercion or intimidation occurs; and
>		2. whether it is, or is a result of, a single incident or is part of an ongoing pattern;
>	4. the person submits to the act because the person is unlawfully detained;
>	5. the person submits to the act because the person is overborne by the abuse of a relationship of authority or trust;
>	6. the person is asleep or unconscious;
>	7. the person is so affected by alcohol or another drug as to be incapable of consenting to the act;
>	8. the person is so affected by alcohol or another drug as to be incapable of withdrawing consent to the act;
>	9. the person is incapable of understanding the sexual nature of the act;
>	10. the person is mistaken about the sexual nature of the act;
>	11. the person is mistaken about the identity of any other person involved in the act;
>	12. the person mistakenly believes that the act is for medical or hygienic purposes;
>	13. the act occurs in the provision of commercial sexual services and the person engages in the act because of a false or misleading representation that the person will be paid;
>	14. if the act involves an animal, the person mistakenly believes that the act is for veterinary or agricultural purposes or scientific research purposes;
>	15. the person engages in the act on the basis that a condom is used and either—
>		1. before or during the act, any other person involved in the act intentionally removes the condom or tampers with the condom; or
>		2. the person who was to use the condom intentionally does not use it;
>	16. having given consent to the act, the person later withdraws consent to the act taking place or continuing.
>2. For the purposes of subsection (1)(m), a false or misleading representation may be made by words or conduct (including by omission) and may be explicit or implicit.
>3. In subsection (1)(m), **commercial sexual services** has the same meaning as it has in [section 53A](https://anzlaw.thomsonreuters.com/Link/Document/FullText?refType=U5&docFamilyGuid=Ib3cbdda6035011e99495db3043f758b0&pubNum=1100190&originationContext=document&transitionType=DocumentItem&docVersion=Law+in+Force&ppcid=278d8fb910c74748a461946a58f14497&contextData=\(sc.DocLink\)) .
