> 1. The credibility rule does not apply to evidence that is relevant to a witness's credibility and that is adduced otherwise than from the witness if—
> 	1. in cross-examination of the witness—
> 		1. the substance of the evidence was put to the witness; and
> 		2. the witness denied, or did not admit or agree to, the substance of the evidence; and
> 	2. the court gives leave to adduce the evidence.
> 2. Leave under subsection (1)(b) is not required if the evidence tends to prove that the witness—
> 	1. is biased or has a motive for being untruthful; or
> 	2. has been convicted of an offence, including an offence against the law of a foreign country; or
> 	3. has made a prior inconsistent statement; or
> 	4. is, or was, unable to be aware of matters to which his or her evidence relates; or
> 	5. has knowingly or recklessly made a false representation while under an obligation, imposed by or under an Australian law or a law of a foreign country, to tell the truth.
