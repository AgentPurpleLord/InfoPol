>(1) Witness **must not**, when giving evidence, **use a document** or try to revive memory about fact/opinion unless court gives leave.
>(2) Court must take into account (in giving leave)-
>	(a) whether witness **able to recall** fact/opinion adequately without document; and
>	(b) whether document witness uses is **copy of document** that-
>		(i) was written or made by witness when events were **fresh in memory**; or
>		(ii) was, at such a time, found by witness to be **accurate** (e.g. **jurat**).
>(3) Witness may read aloud from document with the leave of the court.
>(4) Court can give directions to ensure that only so much as necessary of the document is used.

>[!ABSTRACT] Section 32 Simplified
>- Witnesses cannot use any documents when they're on the stand being questioned to revive their memory.
>- They can use them if the Court grants leave, as in, gives permission to do so.
>- The Court considers whether the witness can recall things, whether the document being referred to was written by the witness at the time of the event, etc. 
>- If Court grants leave, the witness can read from a document.
