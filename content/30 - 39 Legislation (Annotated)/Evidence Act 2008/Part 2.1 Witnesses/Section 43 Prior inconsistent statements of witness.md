>(1) Witness may be cross-examined about [[Prior inconsistent statement]] made by witness whether or not-
>	(a) complete particulars of statement given to witness; or
>	(b) document containing record of statement has been shown to witness.
>(2) If, in cross-examination, a witness does not admit prior inconsistency, cross-examiner is not to adduce evidence of statement otherwise than from witness, unless-
>	(a) informed witness of enough of circumstances of making statement to enable witness to identify statement; and
>	(b) drew witness's attention to so much of statement as is inconsistent with witness's evidence.
>(3) For purpose of adducing evidence of statement, party may re-open party's case.
