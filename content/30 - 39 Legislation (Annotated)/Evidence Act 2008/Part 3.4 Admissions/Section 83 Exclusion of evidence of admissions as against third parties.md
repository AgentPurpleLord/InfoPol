> 1. [[Section 81 Hearsay and opinion rules—exception for admissions and related representations]] does not prevent the application of the hearsay rule or the opinion rule to evidence of an admission in respect of the case of a third party.
> 2. The evidence may be used in respect of the case of a third party if that party consents.
> 3. Consent cannot be given in respect of part only of the evidence.
> 4. In this section, *third party* means a party to the proceeding concerned, other than the party who-
> 	1. made the admission; or
> 	2. adduced the evidence.

>[!IMPORTANT] Meaning
>Admissions by Accused 1 cannot be used against co-accused 2 unless co-accused 2 consents to this. If consent is given, then the admissions and other representations are admitted entirely.
