# Primary Purpose
The Bail Amendment Act 2023's primary purpose is to make changes to:
- Address the remanding of those accused of low-level offending.
- The tests applied to determine a persons bail.
- Change what **Schedule 2 offences** from the current Act. 
- BDM considerations in relation to an Aboriginal person or child.
- **Repeal** the offences:
	- Contravening Certain Conduct Conditions of Bail; and
	- Committing an Indictable Offence Whilst on Bail.
- Modernise/modify sureties to be called 'bail guarantee's or guarantors', replace gendered language, regularise references to bail undertaking and expand circumstances where courts must hear further applications for bail.

# New Section - 4AAA
A new section, 4AAA, has been created (s. 9 Bail Amendment Act 2023), **"Offences in respect of which bail must not be refused"**.
>(1) A BDM must not refuse bail if-
>	(a) person accused only of offences against the **Summary Offences Act 1966** that are not referred to in Schedule 3; and
>	(b) person does not have terrorism record; and
>	(c) if BDM is a court, no exception under (2) applies.
>(2) An exception applies for (1)(c) if-
>	(a) Court determined s.8AA terrorism risk; or
>	(b) person previously granted bail in respect of any of the offences of which the person is accused and that bail was subsequently revoked.

# Schedule 3
A new **Schedule 3** has been created at (s. 11 Bail Amendment Act 2023). It will list offences from the **Summary Offences Act 1966** for which a BDM may refuse bail. These are:
1. Sexual Exposure
2. Common Assault
3. Aggravated Assault
4. Observations of Genital or Anal Region
5. Visually Capturing Genital or Anal Region
6. Distribution of image of Genital or Anal Region
7. Distribution of Intimate Image
8. Threat to Distribute Intimate Image
9. Food or Drink Spiking
10. Public Display of Nazi Symbols
11. Assaulting, etc. emergency workers, custodial officers, youth justice custodial workers or local authority staff on duty
12. Assaulting registered health practitioners
13. Offence to harass witness

# Changes to Unacceptable Risk Test
The **unacceptable risk test** is no longer always applied. It is now applied upon the discharge of the **Step 1-Exceptional Circumstances** or **Step 1-Show Compelling Reason** test.
These changes are located at s. 13, 14 & 15 of the Bail Amendment Act 2023.
The "endanger safety/welfare" clause has also changed in wording:

>endanger the safety or welfare of any other person, **whether by committing an offence that has that effect or by any other means**; or

The combined amendments provide: "prevent a person from being remanded only due to being found to pose an unacceptable risk of committing an offence while on bail" (Explanatory Memorandum for Bail Amendment Act 2023).
