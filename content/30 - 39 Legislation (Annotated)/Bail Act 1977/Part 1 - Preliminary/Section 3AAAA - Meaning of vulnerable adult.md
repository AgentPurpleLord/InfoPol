>1. For the purposes of this Act, a person is a vulnerable adult if the person is 18 years of age or more and has a cognitive, physical or mental health impairments that causes the person to have difficulty in-
> 	1. understanding their rights; or
> 	2. making a decision; or
> 	3. communicating a decision.
> 2. A bail decision maker may consider a person to be a vulnerable adult even if the [[Bail Decision Maker]] cannot identify the particular impairment referred to in subsection (1).


>[!ABSTRACT]+ Section 3AAAA - Simplified
>Person is considered vulnerable adult if:
>- 18 years or older; and
>- **cognitive** impairment; or
>- **physical** impairment; or
>- **mental health** impairment; which
>- causes person to have difficulty in:
>	- understanding **rights**; or
>	- making **decisions**; or
>	- **communicating** decision.
