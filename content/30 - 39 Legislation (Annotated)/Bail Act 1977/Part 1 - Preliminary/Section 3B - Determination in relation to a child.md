>1. In determining in relation to a child, [[Bail Decision Maker]] must take into account:
>	1. the need to **consider all other options** before remand; and
>	2. the need to **strengthen** & **preserve** **relationship** between **child & family**, guardians or carers; and
>	3. desirability of **allowing living arrangements of child to continue** without interruption or disturbance; and
>	4. desirability of allowing **education**, **training** or **employment** of child to continue without interruption or disturbance; and
>	5. the need to **minimise stigma** to child as result from being remanded in custody; and
>	6. **likely sentence** if child found guilty of charged offence; and
>	7. the need to ensure **conditions of bail are no more onerous than necessary** and do not constitute unfair management of the child.
>2. BDM may take into account any recommendation or information contained in report provided by bail support services.
>3. Bail must not be refused solely on ground that child does not have adequate accommodation.
