>1. If this Act provides, in relation to a matter, that a [[Bail Decision Maker]] must take into account the surrounding circumstances, the BDM *must* take into account all the circumstances that are relevant to the matter, including but not limited to, the following—

Please refer to the below table.

| Sub-Section | Circumstance            | Wording                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (1)(aa)     | Likely Sentence         | whether, if the accused were found guilty of the offence with which the accused is charged, it is likely— <br>(i) that the accused would be sentenced to a term of imprisonment; and<br>(ii) if so, that the time the accused would spend remanded in custody if bail is refused would exceed that term of imprisonment;                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| (1)(a)      | Nature of offending     | the nature and seriousness of the alleged offending, including whether it is a serious example of the offence;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| (1)(b)      | Prosecution Case        | the strength of the prosecution case;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| (1)(c)      | Criminal History        | the accused's criminal history;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| (1)(d)      | Bail Compliance         | the extent to which the accused has complied with the conditions of any earlier grant of bail;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| (1)(e)      | Outstanding Matters     | whether, at the time of the alleged offending, the accused—<br>(i) was on bail for another offence; or<br>(ii) was subject to a summons to answer to a charge for another offence; or<br>(iii) was at large awaiting trial for another offence; or<br>(iiia) was on remand for another offence; or<br>(iiib) was at large awaiting sentence for another offence; or<br>(iv) was released under a parole order; or<br>(v) was subject to a community correction order made in respect of, or was otherwise serving a sentence for, another offence; |
| (1)(f)      | Family Violence         | whether there is in force—<br>(i) a family violence intervention order made against the accused; or<br>(ii) a family violence safety notice issued against the accused; or<br>(iii) a recognised DVO made against the accused;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| (1)(g)      | Accused's Background    | the accused's personal circumstances, associations, home environment and background;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| (1)(h)      | Accused's Vulnerability | any special vulnerability of the accused, including—<br>(i) being an Aboriginal person; or<br>(ii) being a child; or<br>(iii) experiencing any ill health, including mental illness; or<br>(iv) having a disability, including physical disability, intellectual disability and cognitive impairment;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| (1)(i)      | Bail Support            | the availability of treatment or bail support services                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| (1)(j)      | Victim Views            | any known view or likely view of an alleged victim of the offending on the grant of bail, the amount of bail or the conditions of bail;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| (1)(k)      | Time in Custody         | the length of time the accused is likely to spend in custody if bail is refused;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| (1)(l)      | Sentence                | the likely sentence to be imposed should the accused be found guilty of the offence with which the accused is charged;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| (1)(m)      | Terrorist Support       | whether the accused has expressed support for—<br>(i) the doing of a terrorist act; or<br>(ii) a terrorist organisation; or<br>(iii) the provision of resources to a terrorist organisation;                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| (1)(n)      | Terrorism Group         | subject to subsection (2), whether the accused has, or has had, an association with—<br>(i) another person or a group that has expressed support of the kind referred to in paragraph (m); or<br>(ii) another person or a group that is directly or indirectly engaged in, preparing for, planning, assisting in or fostering the doing of a terrorist act; or<br>(iii) a terrorist organisation.                                                                                                                                                  |
