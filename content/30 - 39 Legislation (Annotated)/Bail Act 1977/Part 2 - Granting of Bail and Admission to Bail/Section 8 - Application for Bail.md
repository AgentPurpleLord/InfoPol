>1. In any proceedings with respect to bail-
>	1. the bail decision maker is not bound by the rules of evidence;
>	2. the bail decision maker may, subject to paragraph (b), make such inquiries on oath or by affirmation or otherwise of and concerning the accused as the bail decision maker considers desirable;
>	3. the accused shall not be examined
