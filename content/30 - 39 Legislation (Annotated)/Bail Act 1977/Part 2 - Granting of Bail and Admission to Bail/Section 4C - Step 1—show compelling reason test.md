>1. Under 4AA(3) or (4) this section applies.
>	1. BDM must refuse bail unless satisfied of **compelling reason** that justify bail.
>2. Accused bears burden of satisfying BDM of **compelling reason**.
>3. In considering **compelling reason**, BDM must take into account **surrounding circumstances** (s. 3AAA).
>4. If BDM satisfied of compelling reason, must move to step 2—unacceptable risk test.
