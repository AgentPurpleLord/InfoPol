>1. Under [[Section 4AA - When 2 step tests apply|4AA(1) or (2)]] this section applies.
>	1. [[Bail Decision Maker]] must refuse bail unless satisfied that **exceptional circumstances** exist to justify the granting of bail.
>2. Accused bears burden of satisfying BDM as to existence of **exceptional circumstances**.
>3. In considering whether **exceptional circumstances** exist, BDM must take into account **surrounding circumstances** (s. 3AAA).
>4. If BDM satisfied of exceptional circumstances, must move to step 2—unacceptable risk test.


>[!ABSTRACT]+ Section 4A - Simplified
>- A bail decision maker (e.g. magistrate/bail justice), **must** refuse someone bail; when
>- Exceptional circumstances exist; **unless**
>- The accused person can prove they have exceptional circumstances with regard to [[Section 3AAA - Surrounding circumstances]]
