**As defined at rr 15**
A **_vehicle_** includes – (a) a motor vehicle, trailer and tram; and (b) a bicycle; and (ba) an electric scooter); and (c) an animal-drawn vehicle, and an animal that is being ridden or drawing a vehicle; and (d) a combination; and(e) a motorised wheelchair that can travel at over 10 kilometres per hour (on level ground)— but does not include another kind of wheelchair, a train, a wheeled recreational device, a wheeled toy or an electric personal transporter.
