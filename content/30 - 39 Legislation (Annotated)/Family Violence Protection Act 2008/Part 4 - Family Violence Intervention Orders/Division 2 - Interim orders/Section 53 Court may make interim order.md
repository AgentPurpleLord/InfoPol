>1. The court may make an interim order if—
>	1. a person has applied to court for a FV IVO and court satisfied, on balance of probabilities, that IIVO necessary pending final decision about app—
>		1. to ensure safety of AFM; or
>		2. to preserve any property of AFM; or
>		3. to protect AFM who is child who has been subjected to FV committed by RESP; or
>	2. person applied to court for FV IVO and parties to proceeding have consented to, or do not oppose, making of IIVO for app; or
>	3. FVSN has been issued for an AFM and court satisfied, on balance of probabilities, there are no circumstances that would justify discontinuing the protection of the person until a final decision about the app.
>**Note**: See [[Section 81 - Conditions to be included in a family violence intervention order|Division 5]] which provides for inclusion of conditions in a family violence intervention order, including an interim order.
