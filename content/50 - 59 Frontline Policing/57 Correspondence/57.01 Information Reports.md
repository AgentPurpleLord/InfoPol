# Formatting the Body
In general there is no hard rule way to format the body of your IR's. The method I currently use:

> **Context**
> 
> Male source (John BLOGGS) was arrested at Southern Cross Railway Station on the 1st of September 2019. The male approached a member of the public and attempted to sell them marijuana.
> 
> After being interviewed for the offence, I asked BLOGGS for information relating to where he obtains the drugs.
> 
> **Intelligence**
> 
> BLOGGS claims he had only sold locally in Mildura until recently when he came into the city to make a big score. BLOGGS often works with a friend, "Jim" to sell the drugs and split the profits. Jim is normally the one who jumps fences or pries open doors for BLOGGS to get into places. BLOGGS and Jim sell their drugs for $20-$30 for one point, sometimes they will charge $40 to rip off people.
> 
> BLOGGS had approximately 40-45 joints at the beginning of the day and was found with 19 joints when in police custody.
> 
> BLOGGS bought drugs from his "mate's brother" who I believe to be Jim's brother. I inspected BLOGGS phone and found a phone number of 0411 111 111 listed to "Jim". I checked the phone number on LEAP and it linked to Jim FRIEND (MNI: 123456789). He bought approximately 3 - 5 grams of marijuana from the dealer and has then separated the bulk into smaller joints. These have been placed in smaller deal bags. BLOGGS regularly deals to his friends and provides mates rates.
> 
> BLOGGS claimed he has only dealt with his supplier once. He purchased the marijuana from the Mildura Golf Course (1 Mildura Road, Mildura VIC 3000).

As I previously mentioned there isn't one way to go about writing IR's. My goals with the above format were to:

- Provide context for how I came about the intelligence and the source involved.
- Outline a basic level of intelligence gathered.
- Provide any links or beliefs I had about what I was told, based on corroborating whatever intelligence I could.

# Why are IRs Important?
A Police Intelligence 'engine' doesn't run without it's front-line members (or operators). Operators provide valuable intelligence into offenders, victims and crimes. Whether it's street names, tools used or the way someone speaks, any and all intelligence can be valuable.

The information gathered and placed into high quality IR's helps build a web of insight into what's going on and allow decision makers (bosses) to find the best way to respond.

# Submitting an IR
The "subject" field of an IR should focus on significant entities (people) and contain sufficient descriptive information to identify what the IR is about.

E.g. John SMITH - Drug Trafficking Activities in Melbourne Area

# Confidentiality of the Source
A known individual providing information to Victoria Police must either be named in an IR, or registered as a Community or Human Source.

If the author of the IR is concerned about the use of the information or personal approaches to that individual, the narrative should contain:

> "The author of the IR must be contacted prior to actioning the information contained or making any approach to the individual."

# Protective Markings
|Marking|Description|
|---|---|
|**Unofficial**|Non-work related material|
|**Official**|Minor business impact, no approval required, the originating work area remains responsible for the information.|
|**Official: Sensitive**|Limited business impact (personal information, criminal history, business information). Must be destroyed properly (not just thrown out).|
|**Cabinet-in-Confidence**|Major/Serious business impact, for use on Victorian Government Cabinet created documents.|
|**Protected**|Major business impact, valuable and importation information that requires increased protection.|

# Dissemination Codes
1. Can be disseminated to non Law Enforcement Agencies; but must be approved in writing by an Inspector or above.
2. Can be disseminated to all Law Enforcement Agencies and government regulatory bodies without restriction.
3. Can be disseminated to all Law Enforcement Agencies and government regulatory bodies with specific restrictions that can be specified by the completing member e.g. limited dissemination to specified agencies.
4. May be disseminated within Victoria Police only.
5. Cannot be disseminated.
