# Offender Process
Below is a list of each VP Form that should be completed and submitted in relation to an offender being processed. It is broken down by the **result** (ITS, Bail or Remand).

## Intent To Summons

1. **Complete the LEDR Mk2 Report** - This lists the report on LEAP, with your offender being under the "assist enquiry" category.
2. [**VP L14A (Update Offender / Intent to Summons)**](https://victoriapolice.sharepoint.com/sites/intranet-vpm/SitePages/vpl14a.aspx "VP L14A") - This form is used to either **update information** on an offender or to **generate an Intent to Summons report** on the offenders LEAP name.
    1. If you interviewed the accused, make sure you add your **E*JS Attendance No.** and **Interview Date.**
    2. Tick **ITS Interview Date.**
    3. Add the **Sub-Incident No. & Victim Surname** of the above submitted LEDR Mk2 Report.
    4. **Fax** the report to CDEB.
3. [**VP L14B (Offender Antecedent)**](https://victoriapolice.sharepoint.com/sites/intranet-vpm/SitePages/vpl14b.aspx "VP L14B") - You are not required to submit this. However, it creates a new Dossier Report on LEAP, should you feel that be valuable to other members.

## Bail
1. **Complete the LEDR Mk2 Report** - This lists the report on LEAP, with your offender being under the "assist enquiry" category.
2. [**VP L22 (Bail Report)**](https://victoriapolice.sharepoint.com/sites/intranet-vpm/SitePages/vpl22.aspx "VP L22") - This report is generated from a Brief Pack. It will **list the accused's bail** to the submitted LEDR Mk2 Report from above. Ensure you add the **Sub-Incident Number.**
3. **Police/CDEB Charges** - You will have printed 4 copies of Charges. Court/Execution go off to Court. Accused go to the Accused. Police/CDEB remain with you. Add in the **Sub-Incident Number** on the Police/CDEB Charges.
4. **Fax** your L22 and Police/CDEB Charges together, to CDEB.

## Remand
1. **Complete the LEDR Mk2 Report** - This lists the report on LEAP, with your offender being under the "assist enquiry" category. You have a lot going on during a remand, but ensure this gets done.
2. [**VP L14A (Update Offender / Intent to Summons)**](https://victoriapolice.sharepoint.com/sites/intranet-vpm/SitePages/vpl14a.aspx "VP L14A") - This form is used to either **update information** on an offender.
    1. It is also going to be used to **link the offender to the LEDR Mk2 Report** from above. Ensure you put the **Sub-Incident No.** and **Victim Surname** (if available).
    2. **Leave the rest blank.**
    3. Do **not** fax this right away, wait for the next step.
3. **Police/CDEB Charges** - You will have printed 4 copies of Charges. Court/Execution go off to Court. Accused go to the Accused. Police/CDEB remain with you. Add in the **Sub-Incident Number** on the Police/CDEB Charges.
4. **Fax** your **L14A** and **Police/CDEB Charges** together, to CDEB.
    1. This will mark the incident **complete**, as the offender is now linked to the charges.

# Brief Pack Forms
Below is a list of VP Forms that are generated out of a Brief Pack. It's good to understand what each form does and how/when to use them. You should also check the guide to **[Briefs](https://infopol.net/books/4-procedures/page/briefs "Briefs")**.

## Charge Sheet (VP Form 401/402/403)
Your charges form an important part of both your brief and serving the accused (so that he knows what he is being charged with).

There are three VP Forms you can use for your charges, each is used in a different situation:

1. **VP Form 401 (Charge Sheet)** - To be used when the accused is **Bailed**.
    1. **Print** all four copies.
    2. **Sign** and date all four copies on the same date you bail the accused.
    3. **Give** the accused the Accused Copy.
    4. **Send** the Execution/Court Copy to Court.
    5. **Keep** the Police/CDEB Copy.
2. **VP Form 402 (Charge Sheet and Summons)** - To be used when the accused is **Intent to Summons**.
    1. **Print** all four copies.
    2. **Sign** and date all four copies when the charges are Issued (either by yourself or a Registrar).
    3. **Serve** the accused with the Accused Copy.
    4. **Send** the Execution/Court Copy to Court.
    5. **Keep** the Police/CDEB Copy.
3. **VP Form 403 (Charge Sheet and Warrant to Arrest)** - To be used when you are **unable to locate** the accused to serve a Brief on them.
    1. Ensure you enter evidence into the **Warrant Details** section (you should exhaust all avenues of enquiry to locate him and mention them here).
    2. **Tick** what grounds you wish to have the warrant issued.
    3. **Print** all four copies.
    4. **Sign** and date all four copies.
    5. You **must** attend Court to have the warrant issued.
    6. You will then file the Warrant somewhere at your Police Station to be held until the Warrant is executed.

## Bail (VP Form 472 and L22)
The Undertaking of Bail form is the primary document used to initiate bail, meaning, you have a Court Date that the accused is booked in for and you're going to be charging them.

1. **VP Form 472 (Undertaking of Bail)**
    1. Ensure you have the **Court Details, Charges** and **Accused Details** in the Brief Pack. 
    2. Fill in the **Location** (Suburb) and **Date** they will be bailed (today's date).
    3. Fill in the **Before:** details (Sergeant or Bail Justice).
    4. Fill in the **Rank** and **Reg. No** of the person granting Bail if applicable, and tick the right box below.
    5. If you wish to impose any **Crime Conditions** on the bail, fill that in. 
        1. You may want to enforce the accused to reside at their address (so you can serve the brief).
        2. Or to notify you of a change of address within 24 hours.
    6. **Print** the relevant copies (you will rarely require Parent/Surety/Other Person Copy).
    7. **Separate** your copies and have the Sergeant sign off on all of them.
    8. Have the accused **sign** his own Bail.
    9. **Give** the accused the Accused Copy.
    10. **Send** the Original to Court.
    11. **Keep** the Informant Copy for your Brief.
2. **VP Form L22 (Bail)**
    1. Fill in the time that you bailed the accused and put in your Registered Number.
    2. A good practice is to include a brief **Remark(s)** about the circumstances of bail.
    3. **Print** the L22.
    4. Put a **Sub-Incident Number** into the top box. **Do not submit without this!**
    5. Place it on top of your **CDEB/Police Charges** (make sure your Charges also have the Sub-Incident Number).
    6. **Fax** it off to CDEB.

# Minor Forms
These forms are not critical and do not require a lengthy explanation.

- **VP Form 208A (Brief Head)** - Attached to the cover of your brief in order to track the movements of the brief.
- **VP Form 205 (Co-Accused Cover Sheet)** - If you have co-accused(s).
- **VP Form L16 (Charge Record (Traffic))** - For traffic related briefs.
- **VP Form 948 (Diversion Notice)** - If you intend to recommend a Diversion to the court.
- **VP Form 862 (Notice of Lodgment of Certificate(s))** - If you had a drug(s) tested, this should be included in your Brief along with the Analyst's Certificate or Botanist's Certificate.
- **VP Form 950** (Witness Cost / Restitution / Compensation Sheet) - If the accused is required to pay restitution to someone.
- **VP Form 287/287A (Statement Police/Witness)** - If you typed up a statement via the Brief Pack, that's where you can print them.
- **VP Form 1370 (Notice Accompanying Preliminary Brief)** - **[See Briefs and Issue Process](https://infopol.net/books/4-procedures/page/briefs "Briefs")**.
- **VP Form 1372 (Preliminary Brief - Statement Made By Informant)** - **[See Briefs and Issue Process](https://infopol.net/books/4-procedures/page/briefs "Briefs")**.
- **VP Form 1372 (Preliminary Brief - SMBI Remand/Bail Application)** - **[See Remand.](https://infopol.net/books/4-procedures/page/remand "Remand")**
- **VP Form (Details of Witnesses)** - You shouldn't use this form. It provides addresses and phone numbers of all witnesses.

# Person Whereabouts
**[VP L12 (Person Whereabouts Desired) - Fax to CDEB after completing.](https://victoriapolice.sharepoint.com/sites/intranet-vpm/SitePages/vpl14b.aspx "VP L12")**

When you are unable to locate the accused in a matter to have them interviewed, you can place a "Person Whereabouts Desired" on them. This should only be done **after** you have exhausted your avenues to attempt to locate them.

You should discuss the Whereabouts with a Supervisor before completing the form.

The Whereabouts remarks are important. They show up on LEAP and explain to the member that finds the Offender what is going on. Make sure you include:

- Any arrest powers (e.g. 458/459).
- What you need/want done (e.g. interviewed and released pending summons).
- Any relevant files can be located on G: Drive @ this station.

You use the same form when you wish for the Whereabouts to be **cleared** from LEAP.
