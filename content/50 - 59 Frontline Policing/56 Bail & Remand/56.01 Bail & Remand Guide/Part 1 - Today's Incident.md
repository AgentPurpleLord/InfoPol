# Charges

^9cb876

- Write down your charges & number them.
- Review Schedule 1, Schedule 2 and Schedule 3.
- Note which charges today are within the Schedules.

# Evidence

^625bb2

>[!WARNING] Points of proof
>You **must** have evidence satisfying all the points of proof to lay a charge.
>Check [[20 Offences]] for common offences points of proof and defences.

Your evidence might include *statements*, *CCTV*, *physical exhibits* or *photographs/documents*.

Remand applications which you prepare do not need to include *large exhibits* (e.g. lots of photographs). As long as the *important evidence* is in the remand brief in order to satisfy the points of proof, that's all you need.


# Information

^a327a9

- Victim & Witness Details (Name, DOB, Contact Info, Address)
- Arrest, Caution & Rights Time (noted down for the brief)
- Attending Units & Members (noted down for the brief)
- Who did what? Who took photographs, seized an exhibit, arrested the person, etc.
- What else needs to be done before further charges can be laid?

# Reports

^89f694

- LEDR Reports (for sub-incident numbers for the charges)
- IFS (if required)
- PALM (if anything seized to reference on the brief for destruction orders)
