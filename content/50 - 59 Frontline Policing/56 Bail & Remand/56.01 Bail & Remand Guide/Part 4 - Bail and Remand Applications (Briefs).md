>[!TIP] Bail and Remand Applications

# Writing a Bail and Remand Application
The PFSU Remand Example Brief has the following structure for a **statement of alleged facts**:
1. **Special Vulnerabilities of the Accused:** Aboriginal person, child or a vulnerable person and how/why.
2. **Grounds for Opposition of Bail:** Identify / state correct bail disposition.
3. **Current Incident:** A summary as if on a normal brief, cover all points of proof.
4. **Outstanding Matters:** Detail offences, informant name and registered number, main charge, date of offence, disposition for matters, bail conditions, court location and date of hearing.
5. **Unacceptable Risk & Surrounding Circumstances:** FV history (if relevant), other antecedents / information, relevant criminal priors.
