# Seizing Mobile Phone
1. Place Phone into 'Airplane Mode'
	1. IOS = swipe top right corner downwards + press airplane icon
	2. Android = swipe down from top two times + press airplane icon
2. Place device on charge, do not allow to go flat
3. NOTE: If you cannot isolate from network, connect to power bank, place into Faraday bag (or use 3 layers of foil at the least)

# Why This Way?
- Removing the SIM may cause the phone to wipe its data.
- Faraday bags are designed to prevent devices from connecting to any wireless signals or networks.
- If a phone turns off, it might also wipe its data when it gets turned back on.
	- Including if it runs out of battery.
