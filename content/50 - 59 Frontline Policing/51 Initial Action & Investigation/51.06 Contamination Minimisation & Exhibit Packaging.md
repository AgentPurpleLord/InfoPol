# Packaging Exhbits
- Seal exhibits using adhesive taps, never use staples as they are not effective and may cause wounds.
- Label the package with:
    - Description;
    - Location Found (description or address);
    - Collection Members Details (e.g. FC 44425);
    - If item is hazardous, label with 'biological hazard' or 'sharp';
    - Write the label towards the bottom of the bag;
    - Sign across the seal of the bag if you seal it.

If you need to re-open an exhibit for whatever reason:
- Do not break the original seal, cut open the bag below the seal.
- Use gloves and a mask.
- If you must remove the item, place it on a sterile drape.
- Afterwards, return the item to its original bag and make a new seal across the opening.
- Document all dates, times and persons doing the opening for later lodgment.

|Item Type|Advice|
|---|---|
|All Exhibits|Unless special requirements, place them into paper bags with the above instructions. Use TEAB's for Drugs, Jewelry and Cash.|
|Bedding|- Sex offence = mark sheet corner to show which side was upper side and position of foot of bed.<br>- Use a new marker to mark the sheet, or clean end of marker.<br>- Consider any area marked will not have suitable trace DNA.<br>- Paper bag or if large and wet use plastic bag.|
|Cannabis and Oppium|- Whole plants, leave roots intact.<br>- Nursery plants do not remove from the growth medium, keep upright in the tub.<br>- Large plants and loose material = paper bags or hessian bags<br>- Dried material = TEAB<br>- Moldy plant = seal in plastic bag|
|Clothing and Shoes|- Arson, explosive or clan lab residue = arson bag.<br>- Chemical irritant = paper or arson bag.<br>- Collect each item carefully and gently with trace evidence in mind.<br>- Package loosely into bag to allow moisture to evaporate and reduce risk of mold.<br>- You can provide medical staff with the appropriate packaging (if at hospital).<br>- If transporting prior to collection, consider using large sterile drape on the seat.<br>- Use separate areas to collect clothing from suspects and victims, different personal, different car, different rooms, chairs and tables for each person involved. <br>- Take a photograph showing the person wearing the clothing.<br>- Instruct the person to place their own clothing into the bag.|
