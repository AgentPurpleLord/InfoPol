Below is a list of risk factors (they do not indicate family violence) that might predispose a relationship from becoming or being one of family violence.
- Traditional or 'macho' constructions of masculinity exist and are promoted
- Men are 'bread-winners' and women's place is in the home
- Excessive alcohol consumption is encouraged
- Men pressured to conform to notions of toxic masculinity
- Recent separation within the last 2 months
- Substance abuse with drugs or alcohol
- Low levels of educational attainment
- Seasonal changes (more incidents reported in December and January)
- Lack of support services
- Childhood exposure to family violence
