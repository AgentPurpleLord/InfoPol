Public place has wide definitions. It is defined in numerous Victorian statutes. It also has common law definitions.

The onus of proving a place is a public place rests with the Prosecution.

## Sex within view of a public space

R v Clark [2005] 1 SCR 6:

- The accused was masturbating inside his home.
- The light was on and no curtains were in place.
- From the street, the accused could only be seen above the shoulders.
- Not a public place.

Pregelj v Manison (1987) 51 NTR 1:

- Accused and wife having sex in their bedroom.
- Light was on, no curtains in place.
- Both accused and wife thought their sex was private and they couldn't be seen.
- On appeal, it was held that mens rea is an element of hte offence and their conviction quashed.

## Ward v Marsh and Emporium

Ward v Marsh [1959] VR 26 was a matter where the issue was whether the ground floor of the Myer store in Emporium was a public place. The store was a throughfare for the wider shopping centre. At [28]:

> “Public Place” covers every place to which the public qua public may at the relevant time go. It is immaterial that none at such time is present. It is also immaterial how the right of the public to go to such places arises … All that is necessary is that at the time in question members of the public may, because they are members of the public, go to the place if they choose.

And then further at [36]-[37]:
> (a) a “public place” includes a place to which the public generally is in the habit of going in substantial numbers (with or without some limitation of hours) whether by right or not, and whether substantial numbers are actually present at the relevant time or not, provided that is within the limitation of hours (if any) … 
> 
> (b) it includes also a place to which the public is in the habit of resorting as such by reason of its public purpose or character (with or without some limitation of hours), even though not in substantial numbers, whether any of the public are present at the relevant time or not, provided it is within the limitation of hours (if any).
