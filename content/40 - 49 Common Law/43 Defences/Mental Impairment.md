- There is a **two-limb test** that due to the mental impairment the accused:
	- Did not know the *nature* and *quality* of *what they were doing*; or
	- Did not know that their *conduct was wrong*.

# DPP v FB
- FB was charged with 1x murder + 3x attempted murder + 2x reckless conduct endangering life on 8 Sept 2023.
- FB drove his vehicle through Bourke Street Mall, striking three pedestrians who suffered serious injuries. FB then collided with another vehicle, resulting in the death of its driver.
- FB was 28 years old and had a long history of psychotic mental illness, diagnosed specifically with schizoaffective disorder, requiring inpatient treatment.
- During periods where he was not taking medication, he would develop *florid psychotic and manic symptoms*. He was reluctant to take medication due to side effects.
- FB was noted as having delusions of believing he was "a king."
## Knowledge
One of the key limbs of the above test is whether they knew what they were doing was wrong.
