Director of Public Prosecutions v Juchnowski [2008] VSC 181
_Crown appeal — speeding charge dismissed below — proof of speed limit — onus not discharged — annotated certificate — photographs — applicable speed limit proved — annotated photograph proved speed — appeal allowed — Road Safety Act 1986 (Vic), s 83A_

29
[Section 81(2)](https://anzlaw.thomsonreuters.com/Link/Document/FullText?refType=U7&docFamilyGuid=I167f637e045111e99495db3043f758b0&pubNum=1100190&originationContext=document&transitionType=DocumentItem&docVersion=Law+in+Force&ppcid=62e3619873d744a8a21d3954fd87dab8&contextData=\(sc.DocLink\)) provides that “evidence of the speed limit at [the relevant] time and place as indicated or determined on that occasion by an image or message produced by a prescribed process when used in the prescribed manner is … proof of the speed limit on that occasion”. It is clear from these words that Parliament intended that the speed limit can be proved by the relevant image or message. Such evidence is not necessarily conclusive. However, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, it establishes the speed limit.

30
[Section 81(2)](https://anzlaw.thomsonreuters.com/Link/Document/FullText?refType=U7&docFamilyGuid=I167f637e045111e99495db3043f758b0&pubNum=1100190&originationContext=document&transitionType=DocumentItem&docVersion=Law+in+Force&ppcid=62e3619873d744a8a21d3954fd87dab8&contextData=\(sc.DocLink\)) provides that it does not affect any other mode of proof. Accordingly, the prosecution can choose to prove the speed limit by other means. If the mode of proof selected is oral evidence, then in accordance with Ciorra, the evidence must establish the speed limit and the basis upon which it was determined. However, the requirement that the basis upon which the speed limit is determined must be the subject of evidence where proof is provided through oral evidence, does not apply to proof under [s 81(2)](https://anzlaw.thomsonreuters.com/Link/Document/FullText?refType=U7&docFamilyGuid=I167f637e045111e99495db3043f758b0&pubNum=1100190&originationContext=document&transitionType=DocumentItem&docVersion=Law+in+Force&ppcid=62e3619873d744a8a21d3954fd87dab8&contextData=\(sc.DocLink\)). That section operates according to its own terms. Where its requirements are satisfied, the speed limit shown on the image or message must be accepted as being sufficient proof of the speed limit in the absence of evidence to the contrary.
