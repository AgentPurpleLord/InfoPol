[_Crowther v Sala_ (2007) 170 A Crim R 389; [2007] QCA 133](https://anzlaw.thomsonreuters.com/Link/Document/FullText?refType=U2&serNum=2012074440&pubNum=0005341&originationContext=document&transitionType=DocumentItem&ppcid=9554749710344d75ad2182493fffb90c&contextData=\(sc.Keycite\)). There is no implied exclusion of the fault element for s 474.17(1)(b). It must be proved that, objectively viewed, the conduct was menacing and that the defendant either intended that it be so or was reckless as to that fact. In each case both the physical and fault element must be proved.
