# Dangerous Article
- an article which has been *adapted* or *modified* so as to be **capable of being used as a weapon**; or 
- any other article which is carried with the intention of being used as a weapon.

# Controlled Weapons
- A **knife** other than a knife that is a prohibited weapon
- A **spear gun**
- A **baton** or **cudgel**
- A **bayonet**
- A **cattle prod**

## Lawful Excuses
- Pursuit of any lawful **employment**, **duty** or **activity**
- Participation in any lawful **sport**, **recreation** or **entertainment**
- The legitimate **collection**, display or exhibition of weapons.


# Prohibited Weapons
- A sword
- A crossbow
- An imitation firearm
- A dagger
- A martial arts weapon

    - Bo-chuck

    - Butterfly sword

    - Nunchaku
    - Sai or Jitte
    - Shoge
    - Suan Ywe Gou
    - Throwing Star
    - Tonfa
