>*"electric scooter"* means a vehicle designed for use by one person that—
>1. transports a person while the person is either—
>	1. standing on the vehicle; or
>	2. sitting on a seat that is structurally part of the vehicle or purposely built for and correctly installed on the vehicle; and
>2. has **2 wheels** (one in front of the other); and
>3. has a **footboard** between the front and rear wheels; and
>4. is steered by means of a **handlebar**; and
>5. has an unladen mass **not exceeding 45 kilograms**; and
>6. has a maximum speed capability of **25 kilometres per hour** when ridden on level ground; and
>7. can be propelled by both of the following—
>	1. one or more **electric motors**;
>	2. a **person pushing** one foot against the ground.
