>A person is **not** **compellable** to give evidence if the court is satisfied that-
>1. **substantial** **cost** or **delay** would be incurred ensuring person has capacity to understand question or give answer; and
>2. **adequate evidence** on that matter has been given or will be given from one or more other persons / sources.[^1]

>[!ABSTRACT]- Section 14 - Layman's Terms
>A person doesn't need to give their evidence in court if:
>- Giving evidence would have a **big cost or delay** due to that persons ability to understand a question or give an answer; and
>- **Evidence** on the same issue has **already been provided** from someone or something else (document, another witness, etc.)
