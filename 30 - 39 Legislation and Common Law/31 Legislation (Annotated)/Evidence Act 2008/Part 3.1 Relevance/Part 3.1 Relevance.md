# Relevancy Test
Would the evidence, if accepted, could (not would) rationally make a fact in issue more or less likely, however slightly. 

# Inclusionary
The relevancy test is an inclusionary rule. [[Section 56 Relevant evidence to be admissible]] provides that anything which is relevant is to be admissible.

Therefore, if a piece of evidence is relevant and **no exclusionary rules apply**, it must be admitted.
