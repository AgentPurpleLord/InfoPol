[[Circumstantial Evidence]] (not within the Act, but very important)


>[!NOTE] Adduce evidence vs. give evidence
>Parties, or the legal practitioners involved in a matter "adduce" evidence.
>
>Adduce being simply defined is to "cite as evidence", as in "a number of factors are adduced to explain the situation".
>
>Witnesses who are called "give" evidence.
>
>*"The party who is questioning a witness (whether in cross-examination or re-examination) is adducing the evidence at that point in time (ALRC 26 [515])."*[^1]
