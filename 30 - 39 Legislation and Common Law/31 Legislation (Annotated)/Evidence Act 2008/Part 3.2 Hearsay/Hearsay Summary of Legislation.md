# The Hearsay Rule

| Section | Short Title            | Short Meaning                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| s. 59   | Hearsay Rule           | Unless an exception exists, evidence of a previous representation cannot be used to prove a fact.                                                                         |
| s. 60   | Non-hearsay Purpose    | The hearsay rule doesn't apply if it is relevant for a purpose other than proof of an asserted fact.<br>This **does not** apply to an admission in a criminal proceeding. |
| s. 61   | Hearsay and competency | The hearsay rule does not override the competency rule.<br>This **does not** apply to contemporaneous representations.                                                    |


# First-hand Hearsay

| Section | Short Title                              | Short Meaning                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| s. 62   | Restrictions to first-hand hearsay       | A previous representation is considered first-hand if it is something that person saw, heard or perceived, other than a representation made by another person about a fact. |
| s. 63   | Civil proceedings if maker not available | When a person who made a previous representation isn't available to give evidence, the hearsay rule doesn't apply                                                           |
