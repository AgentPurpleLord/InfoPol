> Section 81 does not prevent the application of the hearsay rule to evidence of an admissions unless-
> 1. it is given by a person who saw, heard or otherwise perceived the admission being made; or
> 2. it is a document in which the admission is made.

>[!IMPORTANT] Meaning
>The person who is giving evidence of the admission must have saw, heard or otherwise perceived the admission being made.
>e.g. Dave heard Billy say that he stabbed a person. Dave can give evidence of this admission, because Dave saw/heard Billy saying that.
