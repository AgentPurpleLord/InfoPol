>On hearing an application for a licence eligibility order the Magistrates' Court may make or refuse to make the order, and for the purpose of determining whether or not the order should be made-
>1. the Court must hear any relevant evidence tendered either by the applicant or by the Chief Commissioner of Police and any evidence of a registered medical practitioner required by the Court; and
>2. without limiting its discretion, the Court must have regard to-
>	1. the conduct of the applicant with respect to alcohol or drugs or both during the period of disqualification; and
>	2. the application's physical and mental condition at the time of the hearing of the application; and
>	3. the effect which making the order may have on the safety of the applicant or of the public; and
>	4. any certificate of completion of a first-stage behaviour change program provided to the Court under section 31E; and
>3. if the certificate of completion of the first-stage behaviour chance program indicates that the applicant was referred to a medical or other therapeutic services the court may-
>	1. inquire whether the applicant attended those services; and
>	2. have regard to whether the applicant attended or not.
