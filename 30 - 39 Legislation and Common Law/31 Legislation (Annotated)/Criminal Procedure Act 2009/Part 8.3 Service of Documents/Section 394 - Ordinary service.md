> 1. Ordinary service, unless stated otherwise, is done by—
> 	1. sending a copy by prepaid ordinary post addressed to the person's last known place or residence or business; or
> 	2. if the person is represented by a legal practitioner, sending a copy of the document by prepaid ordinary post to their ordinary place of business; or
> 	3. in any other manner agreed by the parties.


>[!ABSTRACT]+ Section 394 - Simplified
>- If it's acceptable for you to complete service by doing ordinary service (instead of personal service) you can:
>- Send it in the post, or to a legal practitioner in the post, or in any other manner agreed to by the parties involved in the matter.
