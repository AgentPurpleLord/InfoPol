>Unless this Act or any other Act or the rules of court otherwise provide—
>1. if a document is to be served personally, it must be served in accordance with [[Section 391 - Personal service|Section 391]];
>2. if a document is to be served on the informant or the prosecution, it must be served in accordance with [[Section 392 - Service on informant or DPP|Section 392]];
>3. if a document is to be served on a company, registered body, incorporated association or other body corporate, it must be served in accordance with [[Section 393 - Service on company, registered body, incorporated association or other body corporate|Section 393]];
>4. in any other case, a document may be served in accordance with [[Section 394 - Ordinary service]]


>[!ABSTRACT]+ Section 390 - Simplified
>- Service of a document is giving a copy of that document, e.g. a charge or a preliminary brief, to a person.
>- We most often do this when we give a person accused of a crime a preliminary brief of evidence, which outlines allegations against them to be heard in court.
>- Section 390 references four other sections, 391, 392, 393 and 394. These sections provide the rules of **how** we serve the documents.
