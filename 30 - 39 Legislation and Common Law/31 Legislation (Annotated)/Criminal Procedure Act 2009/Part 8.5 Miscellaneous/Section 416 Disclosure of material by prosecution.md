# Explanatory Memorandum
At the time of the Criminal Procedure Bill 2008, s 416 was instead considered to be s 363.
>Clause 363 provides that nothing in this Bill derogates from a duty otherwise imposed on the prosecution to disclose to the accused material relevant to a charge.
