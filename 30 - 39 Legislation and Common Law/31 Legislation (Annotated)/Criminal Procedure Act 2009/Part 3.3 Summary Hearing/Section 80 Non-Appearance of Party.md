>1. If an accused does not appear in answer to a summons to answer to a charge for a summary offence, the Magistrates' Court may—
>	1. if the summons was served in accordance with section 394 (ordinary service), direct that the accused be served personally with the summons; or
>	2. issue a warrant to arrest the accused; or
>	3. proceed to hear and determine the charge in the absence of the accused in accordance with this Part; or
>	4. adjourn the proceeding on any terms that it considers appropriate.
>2. If an accused has been charged with a summary offence and failed to attend in answer to bail, the Magistrates' Court may—
>	1. proceed to hear and determine the charge in the absence of the accused in accordance with this Part; or
>	2. adjourn the proceeding on any terms that it considers appropriate
>3. If the Magistrates' Court proceeds to hear and determine a charge under (1)(c) or (2)(a), the court may dispense with or vary any requirement imposed by or under this Part.
