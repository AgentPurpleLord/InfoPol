>1. BDM must apply **unacceptable risk test** if-
>	1. per s. 4A, BDM satisfied **exceptional circumstances** exist to grant bail; or
>	2. per s. 4C, BDM satisfied **compelling reason** exists to grant bail.
>2. For **unacceptable risk**, the prosecutor bears burden of satisfying BDM of-
>	1. existence of risk mentioned in s.4E(1)(a); and
>	2. that the risk is an unacceptable risk.
>3. On applying unacceptable risk test, BDM must refuse bail if required by s. 4E
