>1. BDM must refuse bail for person accused of **any offence** if BDM satisfied that-
>	1. there is a risk accused would, if released on bail-
>		1. endanger the safety or welfare of any other person, whether by committing an offence that has that effect or by any other means; or
>		2. *repealed*
>		3. interfere with a witness or otherwise obstruct the course of justice in any matter; or
>		4. fail to surrender into custody in accordance with the conditions of bail; and
>	2. the risk is unacceptable.
>2. **Prosecutor bears burden** of satisfying BDM of-
>	1. existence of risk mentioned in (1)(a); and
>	2. that risk is unacceptable risk.
>3. In considering whether risk is unacceptable risk, BDM must-
>	1. take into account **surrounding circumstances** (s. 3AAA); and
>	2. consider whether bail conditions may **mitigate risk** so that it is not unacceptable risk.
