>1. BDM considering release of accused must make inquiries of prosecutor as to whether there is in force-
>	1. FV intervention order against accused; or
>	2. FVSN issued against the accused; or
>	3. recognised DVO against accused.
>2. BDM considering release of accused charged with FV offending must consider-
>	1. if accused released on bail, would there be risk of FV offending; and
>	2. whether risk can be mitigated by
>		1. imposing of conditions; or
>		2. making of a FVIVO.
