>[!IMPORTANT] Key Legislations re: Policing
>An accused on 2 or more counts of bail, who is accused of a Schedule 2 offence, can **only** be bailed by a court.

>1. This section applies to a person (other than a child, a vulnerable adult or an Aboriginal person who is accused of a Schedule 2 offence and who is already on 2 or more undertakings of bail in relation to other indictable offences.
>2. Only a court may grant bail to the person.
