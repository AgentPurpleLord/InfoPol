>1. The step 1—[[Section 4A - Step 1—exceptional circumstances test|exceptional circumstances]] test applies to a decision of whether to grant bail to a person accused of a [[Bail Act Schedule 1|Bail Act Schedule 1]] offence.
>2. The step 1—*exceptional circumstances* test also applies to a decision of whether to grant bail to a person accused of a [[Bail Act Schedule 2]] offence if—
>	1. the person has a terrorism record; or
>	2. the court considering whether to grant bail determines under Section 8AA that there is a risk that the person will commit a terrorism or foreign incursion offence; or
>	3. the offence is alleged to have been committed—
>		1. while the accused was on bail for any Schedule 1 offence or Schedule 2 offence; or
>		2. while the accused was subject to a summons to answer to a charge for any Schedule 1 offence or Schedule 2 offence; or
>		3. while the accused was at large awaiting trial for any Schedule 1 offence or Schedule 2 offence; or
>		4. while the accused was on remand for a Schedule 1 offence or a Schedule 2 offence; or
>		5. while the accused was at large awaiting sentence for a Schedule 1 offence or a Schedule 2 offence; or
>		6. during the period of a community correction order made in respect of the accused for any Schedule 1 offence or Schedule 2 offence; or
>		7. while the accused was otherwise serving a sentence for any Schedule 1 offence or Schedule 2 offence; or
>		8. while the accused was released under a parole order made in respect of any Schedule 1 offence or Schedule 2 offence; or
>	4. the offence is an offence of conspiracy to commit, incitement to commit or attempting to commit an offence in a circumstance set out in paragraph (c).
>3. The step 1—[[Section 4C - Step 1—show compelling reason test|show compelling reason]] test applies to a decision of whether to grant bail to a person accused of a Schedule 2 offence if subsection (2) does not apply.
>4. The step 1—show compelling reason test also applies to a decision of whether to grant bail to a person accused of an offence that is neither a Schedule 1 offence nor a Schedule 2 offence if—
>	1. the person has a terrorism record; or
>	2. the court considering whether to grant bail determines under Section 8AA that there is a risk that the person will commit a terrorism or foreign incursion offence; or
>5. For the purposes of subsection (2)(c)(iiib) and (v)—
>	1. an accused who is released on an undertaking under section 72 of the Sentencing Act 1991 following the court convicting the accused of an offence—
>		1. is not at large awaiting sentence for that offence; and
>		2. is not serving a sentence for that offence; and
>	2. an accused who is released on an undertaking under section 75 of the Sentencing Act 1991 following the court being satisfied that the accused is guilty of an offence—
>		1. is not at large awaiting sentence for that offence; and
>		2. is not serving a sentence for that offence.
>
>In circumstances where a step 1 test applies, section 4D provides that the unacceptable risk test will apply as a step 2 test. However, section 4D also provides that the unacceptable risk test applies even in circumstances where no step 1 test applies.

>[!ABSTRACT]+ Section 4AA Simplified
>1. **Exceptional circumstances** test applies to person accused of [Schedule 1](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch1.html) offence.
>2. **Exceptional circumstances** test **also** applies to person accused of [Schedule 2](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch2.html) offence, if:
>	1. person has **terrorism record**; or
>	2. court considers person under **Section 8AA** a risk of committing terrorism or foreign incursion offences; or
>	3. offence alleged to have been committed-
>		1. while person was on **bail** for any Sch. 1 or Sch. 2 offence; or
>		2. while person was **subject to summons** for any Sch. 1 or Sch. 2 offence; or
>		3. while person at large awaiting trial (**warrant**) for any Sch. 1 or Sch. 2 offence; or
>		4. during period of **community correction order** in respect to any Sch. 1 or Sch. 2 offence; or
>		5. while person was otherwise **serving sentence** for any Sch. 1 or Sch. 2 offence; or
>		6. while person was released under **parole order** for any Sch. 1 or Sch.2 offence; or
>	4. offence is an offence of conspiracy to commit, incitement to commit or attempting to commit an offence in circumstances set out within (c).
>3. Show compelling reason** test applies to person accused of **[Schedule 2](http://www5.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ba197741/sch2.html) offence** if (2) does not apply.
>4. Show compelling reason test applies to person accused of any offence not within Sch. 1 or Sch. 2 if:
>	1. person has terrorism record; or
>	2. court determines under Section 8AA person may commit terrorism or foreign incursion offence.
