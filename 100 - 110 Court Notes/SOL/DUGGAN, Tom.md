# Prosecution Submission
**Sentence**: Head sentence is within range.
- SORA s 1 (purpose); "to require offenders who commit sexual offences to inform police of their whereabouts and personal details" + "to reduce likelihood that they will re-offend"
- *Parsimony*: Mr. DUGGAN given 7x chances with breaching SORA obligations; fines all times; imprisonment is not severe given the priors.
- *Proportionality*: Serious breaches; MO of breaches causes great concern for safety of children in the community; particularly vulnerable children (e.g. DFFH). Imprisonment proportionate to this.
- *Parity*: Previous breaches all relate to failing to notify addresses, or becoming homeless. These relate to child contact. Circumstances wholly different. 
- *Totality*: Overall offending is 12x counts of failing to report name of child contact amongst others. Overall punishment with gravity of offending is within range for imprisonment. 
- *Deterrence*: Both specific and general important there; offenders must know to follow reporting obligations of this nature; Mr. DUGGAN must be made aware of consequences of his actions of this type of breach.
- *Rehabilitation*: Mr. DUGGAN has been in the community for a significant period of time; has continued to breach reporting obligations; limited prospects of rehabilitation.
- *Denunciation*: This type of offending must be condemned; community expects it to be condemned by the courts.
- *Community Protection*: Breaches of this nature could *very* easily lead to further sexual offences against children based on the nature.
