# Bail Position
Your Honour, the accused is an unacceptable risk pursuant to Section 4E of the Bail Act 1977, on the following basis:
- *s. 4E(1)(a)(i) - Endangering the safety and welfare of others*: A number of the outstanding matters include conduct where the accused has set fires during total fire ban days. Those actions have luckily not had disastrous consequences, but represent a real risk to the community at large. Several of these offences were committed whilst he was on bail.
	- For the PHILPOTT matter, a fire was set outside 175 Watton Street in Werribee, which is a medium-high density residential and commercial area. 
	- For the LAY matter, a fire was set outside 70 Alfred Road, Werribee which is a residential area.
	- The guiding principle of the bail Act at s 1B(1AA): The Parliament recognises the overarching importance of maximising, to the greatest extent possible, the safety of the community and persons affected by crime.
	- Prosecution ultimately submit that there are no bail conditions that can ameliorate the risk of the accused setting further fires that would jeopardise the community at large.
- *s. 4E(1)(a)(iv) - Failing to surrender into custody*: The accused has a history of poor bail compliance. The Prosecution case is strong, as the accused has made admissions in each of the matters. The offences carry penalties that Prosecution submit place the accused within range of a combination sentence. 
	- The accused has interstate priors which demonstrate poor bail compliance.
	- The Prosecution case is strong; admissions in all matters.
	- Two outstanding matters (MCDONALD and FLIGHT) were FTAB warrants issues on 20/10/2025.
	- Accused is in real risk of combination sentence; in conjunction with poor compliance and no stable address; no way to ameliorate the risk that the accused would not answer his bail.
