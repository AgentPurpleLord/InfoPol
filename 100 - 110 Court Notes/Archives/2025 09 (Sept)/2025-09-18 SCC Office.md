## Withdrawal Narrative
Full Withdrawal of all charges.

Matter includes significant amount of evidence:
- BWC of several police officers; and
- Statements from numerous victims and witnesses; and
- Several 000 calls; and
- CCTV footage; and
- DVD ROI's of accused and co-accused.

Matter relates to an assault incident occurring in September 2024 at a share house in Wyndham Vale. The share house is occupied by a number of persons. The parties involved are:
- Michael MANDIEKA (victim); and
- Jack PARKER (victim); and
- David RAMOS (accused); and
- John (witness); and
- Ever CARPIO (co-accused) (doesn't reside at this address).

### Background
Prior to this incident, there had been on-going disputes between Michael, Jack and David. Michael and Jack want David to move out from the property due to a disagreement over internal CCTV cameras in David's room.

### Incident
That evening, Michael and Jack approached David demanding he move out of the property. David alleged they did so with machetes, but Michael and Jack made no mention of this to police. 

David left the address after this and called CARPIO complaining about it. CARPIO and his friend (third co-accused) agreed to come and help David move his things out of the property. They returned that same night.

Upon returning, CARPIO stated they found Michael and Jack dancing drunk in the living room. A fight has erupted where Jack put David into a headlock. Michael alleged that he saw a taser fire and ran from the property out the back, jumping fences. Jack alleges that Michael was kidnapped and that he was kicked in the head numerous times by the co-offenders. 

Approximately 8 000 calls occurred shortly after because Michael went around the neighborhood knocking on doors and yelling for help. 

Police have attended shortly after and located Jack at the house and Michael down the street. Statements were obtained from both. Michael provided an inconsistent story on BWC and was later called by a Prosecutor but recalled the incorrect person assaulting him when compared with the statement. 


### Decision
After reviewing all of the evidence, I do not believe that there is a reasonable prospect of conviction. There are the following issues I have identified:
- Jack PARKER's credibility is questionable. He has an extensive family violence perpetrator criminal history. He has an outstanding bench warrant. 
	- Jack's story is inconsistent with the other witnesses.
	- Jack is not supportive of prosecution proceeding.
- Michael MANDIEKA's credibility is questionable. He provided different accounts throughout his interaction with police. He was unable to provide consistent evidence when called by Prosecutors later.
	- Michael admitted that he lied to police about how he ran away from the address.
- John's statement provides the only reasonable account of what occurred. From that account, it is impossible to determine what was occurring. 
	- The only fact that adds to Jack being a victim, is that John alleges one of the males was going through Jack's wallet, however;
	- John had recently woke up, didn't remain out there and could not provide any descriptions of the males.
- CARPIO and RAMOS deny the allegations and create reasonable doubt from their DVD ROI's.

It is on that basis I do not believe that the matter has a reasonable prospect of conviction and should be withdrawn.
