# Charge Amendment Argument
**SUMMARY**: The charge should be amended because it's merely a typo in the charge. The preliminary brief outlines the correct dates, time, locations and particulars. It was served on the accused. 

## 1. Power of the Court to amend
>CPA 2008 Section 8 - Order for amendment of charge-sheet
>(1) The Magistrates' Court at any time may order that a charge-sheet be amended in any manner that the court thinks necessary, unless the required amendment cannot be made without injustice to the accused.

- s. 8(1) Magistrates' + any time + amend + in any manner court thinks necessary + unless amendment cannot be made without injustice to accused.

## 2. What needs to be in a charge-sheet for it to be valid?
>CPA 2008 Schedule 1 
>Clause 1 - **Statement of offence**
>A charge must—
>(a) state the offence that the accused is alleged to have committed; and
>(b) contain the particulars, in accordance with clause 2, that are necessary to give reasonable information as to the nature of the charge.
>Clause 2 - **Statement of particulars**
>1. Subject to (2), particulars of the offence charged must be set out on ordinary language and the use of technical terms is not necessary.
>2. If a rule of law or a statute limits the particulars that are required to be given in a charge, nothing in this clause requires any more particulars than those required.

- Charge sheet complies with Sch. 1 CPA:
	- Sch 1. Clause 1 (a) offence of XPCA within 3 hours is correctly worded/stated.
	- Sch 1. Clause 1 (b) particulars are in ordinary language, but element of time/date is *incorrect*.

## 3. What defects in a charge-sheet cause it to be invalid?
>CPA 2008 Section 9 Errors etc. in charge-sheet
>(1) A charge-sheet is not invalid by reason only of a failure to comply with Schedule 1.
>(2) A charge on a charge-sheet is not invalid by reason only of—
>(a) omitting to state the time at which the offence was committed unless time is an essential element of the offence; or
>(b) incorrectly stating the time at which the offence was committed; or
>(c) stating the offence to have been committed on an impossible day or on a day that never happened.

- s. 9(1) *just because charge sheet failed to comply with Sch. 1 doesn't make it invalid.*
- s. 9(2)(b)/(c) *just because charge sheet has wrong day does not make it invalid either.*

## 4. Does it cause an injustice to the accused?
> CPA 2008 Section 8 Order for amendment of charge-sheet
> (4) If a limitation period applies to the offence charged in the charge-sheet, the charge-sheet may be amended after the expiry of the limitation period if—
> (a) the charge-sheet before the amendment sufficiently disclosed the nature of the offence; and
> (b) the amendment does not amount to the commencement of a proceeding for a new offence; and
> (c) the amendment will not cause injustice to the accused.

- charge's limitation period **is expired** (had until 13/02/2025 to lay correct charge).
- s. 8(4)(a) nature of the offence sufficiently disclosed; (b) not a new offence merely amending the date; (c) prosecution submission it is not injustice due to....

- Fox v DPP (2022) 297 A Crim R 25
	- **Paragraph 28** “Thirdly, s 8(4)(c) permits an amendment to a charge-sheet if (and, we infer, only if) the amendment will not cause injustice to the accused. That is, in our opinion, a ‘catch-all’ provision to ensure that no injustice is occasioned by the amendment. Whether an amendment would cause such injustice will be a question of fact in each case.”
	- **Paragraph 107** “Mr Bant did not allege any injustice pursuant to s 8(4)(c), nor could he. As indicated already, both the infringement notice and the preliminary brief gave notice to Mr Bant of the correct location, and no prejudice arose.”
	- The preliminary brief includes the *analysis date of 16/02/2023* and the *testing date of 13/02/2023*; the particulars are adequately disclosed.
	- Prosecution submissions = **no injustice** due to the material provided being **accurate**, it appears defect within the charge is a **typo**.
