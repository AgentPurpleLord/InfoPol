## Mental impairment
- It is confirmed that SUJKA has schizoaffective disorder.
- There is a two-limb test that due to the mental impairment the accused:
	- Did not know the nature and quality of what they were doing; or
	- Did not know that their conduct was wrong.
- The recent case of DPP v FB [2025] VSC 378 speaks to this test. Essentially, it was found that FB wasn't on his medication for schizoaffective disorder and that was in an acute state of florid psychosis which would have meant he was incapable of knowing what he did was wrong (chargers of murder). 
- This was only as a result of two experts giving evidence as to FB's state of mind in relation to the incident in particular.
