Matter relates to an incident which occurred at approx. 3:00am on 1 March 2023 in the Point Cook area. Burglary has occurred. Owner has detected on CCTV and alerted police. Detectives from Wyndham CIU have attended.

White van was seen leaving address at speed, went through red light, air wing followed to Sunshine. Vehicle was dumped with 3 offenders off on foot. K9 have arrested 1. Detectives arrested another. 3rd male has gotten away and never been arrested.

Defence have raised story that accused was convinced to do a job for cash in hand at a bar and offered to lend his white van to do so. Has also raised that he was under duress during the drive because co-offenders held knives up to him and threatened to hurt him if he didn't keep driving.

No knives were found in the vehicle or on the person arrested. But, the third offender who got away may have had knives. No parties interviewed due to not knowing english. There is no ability or evidence for us to rebut the defence of duress in this case.

No reasonable prospect of conviction. Authorisation to withdraw matter from A/SGT G. STEEL.
