
# Bail Opposition
>Your Honour, bail is opposed.
>There are not show cause tests which apply to the applicant.
>The prosecution submit that the applicant is an unacceptable risk of:

>1. s 4E(1)(a)(i) *endangering safety/welfare of any other person*: The applicant has numerous matters before the court for driving a motor vehicle whilst severely intoxicated. In one of these he failed to stop for police. In another he was involved in a motor vehicle accident. In all of these matters, the accused did not have a current licence. It was cancelled and disqualified during the period of the offending. The applicant has also served previous sentences of imprisonment for driving disqualified. 
>	1. Prosecution submits that there is a significant risk the applicant, if released on bail, will drive a motor vehicle, and that he would do so whilst severely intoxicated. Prosecutions are ultimately concerned he will kill a member of the public on the Victorian Roads if he were to be released on bail.


>2. s 4E(1)(a)(iv) *failing to surrender into custody in accordance with bail undertaking*: The applicant has previously has 69 bench warrants issued for his failure to appear before the courts, dating from 2024 to 2008. On all of the current matters before the courts, he has failed to appear at least once and a bench warrant was issued. Additionally, he has contravened his community corrections order.
>	1. Prosecution submits that there is a significant risk the applicant, if released on bail, will fail to surrender himself into custody.
>	2. Ultimately, the offences before the courts hold terms of imprisonment within range. Sexual assault is the most serious, but given the applicant's history of imprisonment for driving disqualified, all of his charges being considered in totality, a head sentence is within range.
