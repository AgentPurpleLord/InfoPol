# Withdrawal Report
This matter involves 69 charges of Theft - Shopsteal over the course of 6 - 12 months. The accused is well-known amongst the Loss Prevention Officer profession as being a prolific shop-lifter. The 'Auror' platform has begun to use a type of facial recognition identifying system to determine if offenders are linked to reports made via the platform. As such, it appears that numerous reports have been filed at once to Informant LAUDER. 

Numerous Prosecutors have conducted a review of the LAUDER brief. I have also conducted a review of the evidence for each charge. Where the charges were withdrawn, I have noted my reasons on a spreadsheet (attached to the brief); which are limited to withdrawals due to insufficient evidence for a reasonable prospect of conviction. 

Where any charges were withdrawn where enough evidence existed, this was done on a public interest basis that the length of a contested hearing for this matter, as well as the cost to prosecute the accused, far outweighs the value in doing so. A contested hearing would require potentially over 1 week of evidence with numerous LPO's giving evidence.
