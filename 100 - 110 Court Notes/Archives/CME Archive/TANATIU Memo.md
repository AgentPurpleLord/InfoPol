# Joinder
*IF HH desires, I can compile written submissions and have this decided at a Special Mention.*

I raised with defence that the matter should be heard jointly pursuant to s. 57 of the CPA 2009. Defence stated they would oppose this as it would be prejudicial. My argument for the joint hearing:
- Conduct in both matters is against Jade LLOYD, the same victim.
- Conduct in both matters relates to the same intervention order being breached.
- A joint hearing would prevent both the victim and the serving member for the intervention order providing duplicitous evidence.
- A joint hearing would streamline voir dire's regarding hearsay evidence. The victim may lodge a s 18 EA application and defence would contest admissibility of DREC under s 56 EA. This would need to be heard twice in separate matters.
