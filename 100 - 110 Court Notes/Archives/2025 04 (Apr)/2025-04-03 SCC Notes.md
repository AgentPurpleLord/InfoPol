**Reasonable Prospect of Conviction**
The only evidence present within the brief is CCTV and a DVD ROI.
The CCTV is conceded by the accused and ID is admitted to.
Given that it is an attempted offence, we would not need to prove that the accused entered a building, but that he had attempted to enter a building. This is proven by way of the CCTV; accused can be seen attempting to open a sliding door.
However, we do not have evidence to prove the intent in the accused mind at the time of attempting to open the door. The DVD ROI does not include admissions to intent (accused stated he could not remember).
Interestingly, the matter for GRENFELD included an offence against the same property as in this one. At that time, the accused stated on his DVD ROI that he went to this property (which is a hair salon) because he was homeless and cold. When he realised the door was unlocked, and looked inside to see the hair salon items, he closed the door and left. He believed it looked abandoned from the outside.
There is any chance, given the accused low IQ and mental health difficulties, he merely forgot he had previously gone into this property and what had occurred. It is also a steep hill to climb to use a DVD ROI from a different matter for this matter.
It is based upon those reasons I do not believe we can prove the offences beyond a reasonable doubt.
