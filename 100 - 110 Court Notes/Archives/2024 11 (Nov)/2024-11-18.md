Charges:
1. Theft Shopsteal (PG)
2. Unlawful Assault (PG)
3. Threaten to Injure Person Lawfully Detaining Another (WD)
Matter being withdrawn on the basis that we cannot prove charge beyond a reasonable doubt. Elements of the charge require that a person is being lawfully apprehended. The SOAF provides that the accused was not being apprehended by the LPO's, the LPO's were asking that the accused return the stolen items in her trolley. The accused has then made that threat.
Accused was represented (did not get details, this is my first WD report, be kind please), nil costs awarded. ROUTINE.

The appropriate charge, being Unlawful Assault, has already been laid by the informant. Matter to proceed on that charge as it is appropriate re: the belief of threat / force upon them by the accused.

Key point in issue is the nature of the threat that was made. The LPO was not attempting to apprehend the offender, the LPO was only attempting to get the good back. The LPO has grabbed the trolley of the accused's trolley and asked him to take out the goods that were not paid for. Then the accused has threatened him with a syringe.
To withdraw charge 3 (being charge of threatening to injury a person to avoid apprehension) and for matter to proceed on a plea of guilty to unlawful assault in relation to the threat of stabbing the LPO with a syringe made by the accused. This charge is the most appropriate in all of the circumstances.
