# DPP (Cth) v Garside (2016) 50 VR 800
At [25]:
- (a) Unless exceptional circumstances exist, a sentence involving an immediate term of imprisonment is ordinarily warranted.
- (b)(i) nature and content of material, age of children, gravity of sexual act.
- (b)(ii) number of items or images possessed
- (b)(iii) whether material for sale or distribution
- (b)(iv) whether profit from offending
- (b)(v) number of children depicted
- (b)(vi) the length of time for which the pornographic material was possessed.
- (c) general deterrence is the primary sentencing consideration
- (d) Less weight or limited weight is given to offender's prior good character.
- (e) CAM occurs internationally, prevalent re: internet.
- (f) Offending involving CAM is difficult to detect given anonymity re: internet.
- (g) Possession of the material creates market for the continued corruption and exploitation of children.
- (h) Paramount public interest objective, promoting protection of children, not victimless crime.
- (i) The fact that an offender does not pay to access a child pornography website or was not involved in the distribution or sale of child pornography does not mitigate the offending.
