## Grounds of Bail Application
Bail Application is made via s. 18(1) and CPA s. 265.
>If an application is made under subsection (1), the Magistrates' Court must either grant or refuse bail as if the appellant were accused of an offence and were being held in custody in relation to that offence and, for this purpose, the Bail Act 1977 (with any necessary modifications) applies.

If the matter is to be heard by the courts, pursuant to s. 18AA:
- It needs to either be the first or second time the applicant is trying for bail since being taken into custody; or
- the applicant has new facts/circumstances since the last refusal of bail; or
- applicant was not repped in the last bail app; or
- the order was made by bail justice.

And pursuant to s. 18AB the hearing must be conducted fresh.
