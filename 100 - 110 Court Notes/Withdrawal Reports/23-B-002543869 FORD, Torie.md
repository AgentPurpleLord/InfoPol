# Withdrawal Narrative
1 - Resist Police - W/D
2 - Possess Cannabis - P/G
3 - Possess on Court Premises Offensive Weapon - P/G
4 to 7 (W/D and rolled into 3)
8 - Possess Item Prohibited at Magistrates' Court - PG
9 to 12 (W/D and rolled into 8)

Matter involves the accused attended the Werribee Magistrates' Court on 30/01/2023. Accused was homeless at the time. Accused went through G4S security with a number of prohibited items in her possession. G4S alerted Court Security who have gone out to speak with the accused. Accused became upset when informed the items weren't going to be returned to her (as one of the items was sentimental). Accused taken to ground shortly after by members.

Accused was being quite confrontational and insulting members after being taken to the ground. 

A man fleeing from police officers who wanted to question him about leaving a restaurant without paying his bill was not guilty of resisting the police prior to any arrest, because until arrested there was no legal obligation for him to stop when requested or to answer questions: Director of Public Prosecutions v Hamilton (2011) 33 VR 505; 214 A Crim R 432; [2011] VSC 598

Given these circumstances, the accused sitaution is of similar likeness. There is no reasonable prospect of conviction as we do not have any evidence that the accused was actively resisting police.

All other charges proceeded as rolled into each other. In relation to Charge 3, HH KEIL specifically ordered not to destroy the item 202301-M-2730-0003 which is a knife of sentimental value. This is reflected on the brief. 

Spoke with A/SGT TURNER regarding the authorisation of W/D.
