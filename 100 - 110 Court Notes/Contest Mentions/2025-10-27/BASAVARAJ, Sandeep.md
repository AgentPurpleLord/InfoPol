Charge 1 - Contravene Cause Harm/Fear - PG
Charge 2 - Contravene Cause Harm/Fear - PG
Charge 3 - Telecommunications to Harass - PG
Charge 4 - Unlawful Assault - W/D

On the night, the victim alleges that she had an argument over the mortgage with her drunk husband at home. She states they were both hitting each other during the argument, but the drunk husband was the instigator.

The victim ran out of her house and left her son at home with him. She called the police, who attended and took a DREC in the car-park of KMART. 

The accused was arrested and issued with a FVSN. At 3am, police re-attended when he had breached the order and obtained another DREC on the victims front porch. I am not sure why police in this matter decided taking DREC's outside twice was the best way to do take a DREC... 

I contacted the victim on 22/10/2025, she was uncertain about giving evidence and proceeding. She stated she does not wish to cause any more harm to the accused than she already has. I re-iterated that nothing was her fault. She was entrenched in not making a decision about the matter and stated she wanted more time to decide.

The informant has not complied with numerous requests for disclosure made to him. No full brief has been compiled. No 000 call has been obtained. No medical records were obtained. 

Given all of this and defence offering to resolve on the above, I believe there is no reasonable prospect of conviction and that Charge 4 should be withdrawn so the matter resolves. We are also avoiding potential non-disclosure costs due to the informants inaction.
