# Prepared Withdrawal Narrative (if required)
Charge 1 - Theft - W/D

Matter relates to an enigmatic offence that could arguable be a OPBD or Theft. In December 2019, the victim stated he gave $37,000 to the accused with an unwritten agreement in place; for that money to be transferred to the victims family in Iran through the accused's money transfer business. 

Shortly after making this transaction, the victim went to confirm with the accused that he had gotten the money. The accused denied any transaction being sent through. The victim went to his bank and the accused's bank and was not able to confirm the transactions or get his money returned.

In May 2020, the victim reported the matter to the informant at Werribee Police Station but declined to make a statement in relation to it. The victim went back to the accused, who allegedly showed him a bank statement which *did not* include the money transferred in. 

In August 2020, the victim returned to the informant and provided a statement in relation to the matter. 

Police completed a bank warrant with WestPac and obtained bank records for the accused. The bank records confirmed that the transactions had occurred. Shortly after the transactions, the money was transferred out to the account to numerous other accounts.

In December 2020, the accused was arrested and interviewed. The accused essentially provided the same story. There was an agreement, but the money never came through. He provided police in the interview with a bank statement that did not include the transactions received. When challenged with the records which did contain them, the accused stated another person has access to the account. The accused provided a name on ROI, but did not provide any contact details. Of note, on the ROI the accused states he will provide the informant with a phone number post-interview.

It remains unknown whether this avenue of enquiry was followed. The 'doctored' bank statement was never obtained by police. It was handed back to the accused. The informant remains on long-term leave and can't provide anything to us. The corroborator states the offence is so old that he does not recall any details about it. The LEAP investigation notes make no reference to the nomination by the accused and this third-party who the accused alleged was behind the offending. 

This matter therefore falls into an awkward category. We are unable to determine if the avenue of the third-party was followed up. The informant is on long-term leave for the foreseeable future. Where we cannot lead the evidence to counter-act this defence, I do not believe there is a reasonable prospect of conviction.
