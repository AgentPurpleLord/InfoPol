ii. In applying the cases of R v De Montero (2009) 25 VR 694; R v Guthridge (2010) 27 VR 452; McBride v The Queen (1966) 115 CLR 44; King v The Queen (2012) 245 CLR 588, dangerous driving will only be satisfied if the speed or manner in which our client drove posed a real, and not just speculative, danger to other members of the public who may have been in the vicinity.
