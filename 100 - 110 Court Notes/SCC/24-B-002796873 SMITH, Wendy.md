# Withdrawal Narrative
Charge 1 - Criminal Damage - W/D
Charge 2 - Aggravated Assault - W/D

Matter relates to an incident at an NDIS care house in which the accused resides with full time carers. Accused became upset and argued with a carer. This resulted in the accused smashing a glass and using a glass shard to harm herself. The accused spat in the direction of the victim re: assault but the spit did not make contact. 

Police have attended and arrested the accused. The accused was taken to Werribee Police Station and deemed not fit for interview. 

Defence have provided materials regarding mental impairment. This material makes it abundantly clear that the accused is not mentally capable of forming a mens rea. 

Given that a mental impairment defence has been raised with this material, I do not believe it is appropriate for the accused to be charged as there is not a reasonable prospect of conviction in the matter. I have sent emails to the authorising Sergeant and Informant seeking their views but have not currently heard back from them. I have discussed the matter with A/SGT TURNER who has agreed with my assessment to withdraw the matter.
