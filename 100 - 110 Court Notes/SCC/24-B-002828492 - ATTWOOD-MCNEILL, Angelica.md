# Withdrawal Narrative
Charge 1 - Contravene FVSN - PG
Charge 2 - Unlawful Assault - W/D

Matter is a family violence matter involving two parties assaulting each other near the Tarneit Railway Station. The incident was observed by onlookers who called 000, but did not remain on the scene. 

The accused was field interviewed and claimed she was on the ground and elbowed the victim in the face in self-defence. The victim refused to provide an account to police. Further checks via LEAP identified that the victim has been charged with RCI, MTTK, persistent contravention and contravention FV IVO intent to cause harm/fear against the accused for this occasion, suggesting potential misidentification factors at play.

There is no reasonable prospect of conviction in this matter. There are no statements on the brief of evidence. A review of LEAP show the informant was going to follow-up with obtaining statements from the 000-callers but has not done so since August 2024. There are availabilities of defence (self-defence) for the accused, which cannot be disputed without a victim statement. The victim was non-compliant with police and did not provide a statement. 

I have therefore withdrawn the Unlawful Assault charge.
