**_‘Without Prejudice Offer to Resolve the Matter’_**

We refer to the matter of Mr Shiva SHAGANTI listed at the Werribee Magistrates’ Court for mention on 3 April 2025. We confirm we act on behalf of Mr Shaganti who is charged as follows:

**_Charge 1 – Unlawful Assault_**

Accordingly, we have reviewed the preliminary brief of evidence and taken our client’s instructions. We seek that you consider Mr Shaganti suitable for a diversion notice in regard to this matter. The reason being – the assistance he has provided Police in making a statement in regard to the activities of himself and the co-accused on the said day the allegations occurred.

Mr Shaganti is 32 years of age with no prior history of offending. On this occasion, he has cooperated with Police by making a statement which is durated – providing information of what has occurred. Should Mr Shaganti be considered for diversion, the Police will be able to make recommendations of the conditions they would deem appropriate to become part of the Diversion Plan – such as to be of good behaviour, to pay a fine, to write a letter of apology to the victim, write a letter of gratitude to the Informant/Prosecution and to undertake any courses. A diversion plan would encourage Mr Shaganti to comply and undertake completion of all the conditions in an effort to keep his record clear.

Should you not accept our offer, we confirm we hold instructions to adjourn the matter to a contest mention. We will then be seeking a full brief of evidence as mandated by section 39 of the Criminal Procedure Act 2009.

It is further requested, pursuant to section 42 of the Criminal Procedure Act 2009, that any further documentation which becomes available, or is brought into existence after we have been supplied with the above preliminary brief, is served on this office as it becomes available. 

Thank you in advance for considering the above. Please do not hesitate to contact the writer with any questions or concerns on 0429 229 525 or alternatively on (03) 9943 3989.
