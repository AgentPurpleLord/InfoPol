# Offences & Elements
## ICI / RCI
1.  Identity
2.  Injury
3.  Causation (did the accused cause the injury)
4.  Intention or Reckless
5.  Lawful Justification or Excuse

## Affray
1. Identity
2. Used / threatened unlawful violence
3. Intention / Reckless to use of violence or threat of violence
4. Bystander of reasonable firmness might be terrified to see.
