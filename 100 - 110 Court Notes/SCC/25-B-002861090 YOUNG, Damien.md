# Withdrawal report
Charge 1 - Criminal Damage - W/D

Matter is a single charge of criminal damage. Circumstances of the case are that the accused has used a permanent marker to write on an interior wall of KFC. The contents of the writing are in paragraph format and are a stream of conciousness around the accused's 'stolen money'. This was raised to police attention by passerbys.

Upon review of the brief of evidence and at SCC, it was raised that the accused has an outstanding arson charge before higher courts. A medical report has been prepared for that matter by a psychiatrist and speaks to the offending in this matter as well. 

Based upon the combination of that report and the element of criminal damage being that of intent needing to be satisfied, I do not believe there is a reasonable prospect of conviction. There is a possible defence that could be raised in the matter, whereby the accused can argue he never intended to permanently damage the wall. There are also issued that the KFC staff appear to have been able to wipe away the marker with a dry cloth shortly after it was marked onto the wall. The wall material itself was tiling, which are easy to clean of permanent marker. 

Given these factors and after discussing the matter with A/SGT TURNER; I believe the matter needs to be withdrawn as there are no reasonable prospects of a successful prosecution.
