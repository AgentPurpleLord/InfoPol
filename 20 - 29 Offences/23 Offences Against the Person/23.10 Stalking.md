>[!info]
>[s. 21A Crimes Act](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s21a.html "Stalking") — A **person must not stalk another person**.

>[!success]
>The Victorian Judicial College has a [Charge Book section](http://www.judicialcollege.vic.edu.au/eManuals/CCB/index.htm#46773.htm "Charge Book - Stalking") relating to this matter.

# Example
Cindy begins to stalk someone she has romantic feelings for by following them during their daily routine, emailing them repeatedly, loitering outside their workplace and home. The person she is attempting to speak to does not reciprocate the sentiment and asks Cindy to stop, however, she continues ignoring the person.

2.  An Intentional [[Course of Conduct]]
