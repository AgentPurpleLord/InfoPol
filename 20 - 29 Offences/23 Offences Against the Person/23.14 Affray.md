>[!INFO] Seciton 195H Affray
>1. A person who uses or threatens unlawful violence and whose conduct would cause a person of reasonable firmness present at the scene to be terrified commits an offence and is liable to—
>	1. level 6 imprisonment (5 years maximum); or
>	2. imprisonment for 7 years if, at the time of committing the offence, the person is wearing a face covering used primarily—
>		1. to conceal the person's identity; or
>		2. to protect the person from the effects of a crowd-controlling substance.
