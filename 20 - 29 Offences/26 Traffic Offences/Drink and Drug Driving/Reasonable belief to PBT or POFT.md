The prosecution must prove the requisite belief etc was held based on reasonable grounds to prove the relevant element of the offence. These qualifications to the discretionary investigative powers are states of mind required by the investigator or expert witness to enable them to require a certain action or reach a certain conclusion. What is required of the investigator is a conscious appraisal of the situation and information available so as to satisfy themselves there is a basis for making a requirement of a person or reaching a conclusion about the subject person's response.
