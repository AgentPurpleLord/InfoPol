>[!INFO]
>[Section 75 Drugs, Poisons and Controlled Substances Act](http://austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/consol_act/dpacsa1981422/s75.html "Section 75 Drugs, Poisons and Controlled Substances Act") - A **person**, without being authorized or licensed under this Act; **uses** or **attempts** to use a **Drug of Dependence**.

# Example
Dave is seen sniffing a line of cocaine, but no cocaine is found in Dave's [[possession]] when he is later searched by Police.

# Power of Arrest
[Section 458 of the Crimes Act.](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s458.html "Section 458 of the Crimes Act.")

# Jurisdiction
Summary Offence #summary-offence  heard at the Magistrates' Court.

# Points of Proof
1. Identity
2. Use Drug of Dependence or Attempt To Do So
