>[!info]
>[Section 71AC of the Drugs, Poisons and Controlled Substances Act](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/dpacsa1981422/s71ac.html "Section 71AC of the Drugs, Poisons and Controlled Substances Act") - A **person** who, without being authorized or licenced under this Act; **trafficks** or **attempts** to traffick a **Drug of Dependence**.

>[!success]
>The Victorian Judicial College has a [Charge Book section](http://www.judicialcollege.vic.edu.au/eManuals/CCB/index.htm#19310.htm "Charge Book - Traffick DoD") relating to this matter.

# Example
Fred is reported by a member of the public for suspicious activity. He is seen in his car with multiple people walking up to the window.

# Power of Arrest
[Section 459 of the Crimes Act.](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s459.html "Section 459 of the Crimes Act.")

# Jurisdiction
Indictable Offence #iots heard at the Magistrates' Court.

# Defences
A person may be authorised or licensed under the Drugs, Poisons and Controlled Substances Act or the Access to Medical Cannabis Act 2016.

# Points of Proof
1. Identity
2. Is the substance a Drug of Dependence?
3. Is the person in [[possession]] of the Drug of Dependence?

# Charge
The accused at **PLACE** on **DATE** did **TRAFFICK A / ATTEMPT TO TRAFFICK A DRUG OF DEPENDENCE** **NAMELY DRUG**.
