>**Section 51G Crimes Act 1958**
>(1) A person (A) commits an offence if A knowingly possesses child abuse material.
>**Level 5 Imprisonment (10 years maximum)**


# Elements of the offence
1. The offence occurred at the time and place alleged.
2. The offender was the accused.
3. The accused had in his or her possession, child abuse material.
4. That the accused knew that they had in their possession child abuse material. 
5. That the accused knew or believed that the material in their possession described or depicted a person who is, or who appears or is implied to be, a child as a victim of torture, cruelty or physical abuse; or in the presence of another person who is engaged in, or apparently engaged in, a sexual pose or sexual activity; or the genital or anal region of a person who is, or who appears or is implied to be, a child; or the breast area of a person who is or who appears to be or is implied to be a female child; and reasonable person would regard as being, in the circumstances, offensive.
