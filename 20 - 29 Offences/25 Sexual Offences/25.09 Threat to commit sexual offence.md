(1) A person (A) commits an offence if—
	(a) A makes to another person (B) a threat to rape or sexually assault B or a third person (C); and
	(b) A intends that B will believe, or believes that B will probably believe, that A will carry out the threat.

# Charge Wording
AB at .......... on the .......... day of .......... 20 .......... made to CD a threat to rape [sexually assault] CD (EF) intending that CD would believe [believing that CD would probably believe] that the said threat would be carried out.

# Elements
1. That the offence occurred at the place and time alleged.
2. That the offender was the accused (or that he or she was an accomplice)
3. That the accused made a threat to another person.
4. That the threat was to rape or sexually assault that person or some other person.
5. That the accused:
	1. intended that the other person would believe that the threat would be carried out; or
	2. believed that the other person would probably believe that the threat would be carried out.

# Threat
Section 43(2) provides:
>(2) Words or conduct may constitute a threat for the purposes of (1) if by those words or conduct an intention to do any of the following is conveyed—
>	(a) to sexually penetrate or sexually touch B or C without B or C's consent;
>	(b) to cause B or C, without B or C's consent, to sexually penetrate or sexually touch—
>		(i) A; or
>		(ii) C or B (as the case requires); or
>		(iii) themselves; or
>		(iv) another person; or
>		(v) an animal;
>	(c) to cause B or C, without B or C's consent, to be sexually penetrated or sexually touched by another persons or by an animal.



# Explanatory Memorandum
Crimes Amendment (Sexual Offences and Other Matters) Bill 2014
>New section 43—threat to commit a sexual offence 
>New section 43 provides for the offence of threat to commit a sexual offence. 
>New section 43(1) provides that a person (A) commits an offence if—
>- A makes to another person (B) a threat to rape or sexually assault B or a third person (C); and 
>- A intends that B will believe, or believes that B will probably believe, that A will carry out the threat. 
>New section 43 replaces the existing offence of assault with intent to rape in current section 40 of the Crimes Act 1958, which includes threats to assault with intent to commit rape. 
>New section 43(2) provides that words or conduct may constitute a threat for the purposes of subsection (1) if by those words or that conduct an intention to do any of the following is conveyed— 
>- to sexually penetrate or sexually touch B or C without B or C's consent; 
>- to cause B or C, without B or C's consent, to sexually penetrate or sexually touch—
>	- A; or
>	- C or B (as the case requires); or
>	- themselves; or 
>	- another person; or 
>	- an animal. 
>New section 43(3) provides that the maximum penalty for the offence of threat to commit a sexual offence is level 6 imprisonment (5 years maximum).
>New section 43(4) provides that a threat may be made by any conduct and may be explicit or implicit. 
>The note to new section 42 provides that an offence against subsection (1) may be heard and determined summarily by the Magistrates' Court if section 29 of the Criminal Procedure Act 2009 is satisfied.
