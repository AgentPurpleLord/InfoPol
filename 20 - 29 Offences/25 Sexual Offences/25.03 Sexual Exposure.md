>[!info]
>[Section 19 Summary Offences Act](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/soa1966189/s19.html "Sexual Exposure") - A person (A) commits an offence if; (A) **exposes** (to any extent) (A's) **genitals**; and (A) intends to expose their genistals. The exposure must be **sexual** and is in or within view of a **public place**.

# Example
A park-fiend regularly goes around and exposes his penis to passer-by's in a public park, with the intent for sexual gratification.

# Power of Arrest
[Section 458 of the Crimes Act.](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s458.html "Section 458 of the Crimes Act.")

# Jurisdiction
Summary Offence heard at the Magistrates' Court.

# Defences
**Mistaken** but honest and reasonable belief that the exposure was **not** 'in' or within view of a public place.

# Point of Proof
- Identity
- No Consent Given
- Exposes Genitals (to any extent)
- Intends to Expose Genitals
- Exposure is Sexual
- Exposure is in or Within View of Public Place
