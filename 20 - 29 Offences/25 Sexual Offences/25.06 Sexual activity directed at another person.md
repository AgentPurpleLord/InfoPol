>(1) A person (A) commits an offence if—
>	(a) A engages in an activity; and
>	(b) the activity is sexual; and
>	(c) another person (B) sees the activity or a part of the activity; and
>	(d) A knows that B will see, or will probably see, the activity or a part of the activity; and
>	(e) A—
>		(i) intends that B will experience fear or distress from seeing the activity or a part of the activity; or
>		(ii) knows that B will experience, or will probably experience, fear or distress from seeing the activity or a part of the activity.

# Wording of charge
AB on the .......... day of .......... 20 .......... engaged in a sexual activity knowing that CD will (probably) see the activity (a part of the activity) and intending (knowing) that CD will (probably) experience fear or distress from seeing the activity (a part of the activity), and CD sees the activity (a part of the activity).

# Elements
1. That the offence occurred at the *place* and *time* alleged.
2. That the offender *was the accused*.
3. That the accused engaged in *sexual activity*.
4. That another *person (B)* saw the *activity* or a *part of the activity*.
5. That the accused knows that *person (B)* *will see*, or *will probably see*, the activity or part of the activity.
6. That the accused either *intends* or *knows* that *person (B)* *will experience (or probably will experience) fear or distress from seeing the activity or part of the activity*.
