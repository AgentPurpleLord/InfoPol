>[!INFO]
>[[Section 9 Wilful destruction, damage, etc. of property|Section 9]] of the Summary Offences Act 1966
>Any **person** who **[[Willful| wilfully]]** injures or **[[Damage or Destroy | damages]]** any **[[20 - 29 Offences/20 Meta/20.01 Offence Definitions/Property| property]]**, with the value being under $5,000.

# Example
A person throws a rock at a bird in order to scare it away, but misses the bird and hits a window. They have committed [[Willful|willful]] damage onto the window.

# Power of Arrest
[Section 458 of the Crimes Act.](http://www8.austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/vic/consol_act/ca195882/s458.html "Section 458 of the Crimes Act.")

# Jurisdiction2
Summary Offence #summary-offence  heard at the Magistrates' Court.

# Where Committed
In a public place or on private property.

# Defences
Not committed if the person acts under fair and reasonable supposition they had a right to the act. E.g. Tree surgeon requested by owner of private property to remove trees. During removal, he removes one extra one due to direction from second female owner.

# Point of Proof
- The offence occurred at the *place* and *time* alleged.
- The offender *was the accused* (identity).
- The accused *willfully injured* or *damaged property*.
- The value of the damage was *less than $5000*.
- Identity
- [[Willful]] Action (intent or reckless to the damage caused)
- [[Damage or Destroy |Damage]] to a piece of [[20 - 29 Offences/20 Meta/20.01 Offence Definitions/Property]]

# Charge wording
The accused at [place] on [date] wilfully [injured/damaged] property, namely [description of property], and the value of that damage was [value of damage].
