# Prohibited Person
- Serving term of imprisonment for;
	- Indictable offence; or
	- Assault; or
	- Drugs; or
	- Weapons
- Previously served 5 years for one of the above and not more than 15 years have expired since; or
- Previously served less than 5 years for one of the above and not more than 5 years have expired; or
- A person who is subject to a final intervention order whether or not there are conditions relating to a licence; or
- subject to a CCO with a supervision condition; or
- a person who had one of the above orders and not more than 5 years have expired since the end of the order; or
- a person guilty of Firearms offence that carries term of imprisonment; or an offence where firearms were used and carries imprisonment; or indictable offence and not more than 12 months have passed since person found guilty.
