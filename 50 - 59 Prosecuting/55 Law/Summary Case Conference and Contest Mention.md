# Overview
A matter must progress in the following manner:
1. *s. 6 CPA*: The filing of a charge-sheet with the Registrar of the Magistrates' Court begins the proceeding.
	1. *s. 7 CPA*: Summary offences have 12 months. Indictable offences have as long as they want.
2. *s. 53 CPA*: A mention hearing is held, with the following options:
	1. *53(a)*: if IOTS -> grant summary hearing; or
	2. *53(b)*: proceed to hear + determine charge; or
	3. *53(c)*: fix date for contest mention hearing; or
	4. *53(d)*: fix date for summary hearing of charge; or
	5. *53(e)*: make any other order or give any direction the court considers appropriate.
3. *s. 54(2) CPA*: If a preliminary brief is served within 21 days of the charge-sheet filing date, a SCC **must** be conducted before the charge can be set down for a Contest Mention Hearing or Summary Hearing; or a full brief request.
