- An application is made under s 88 of the Criminal Procedure Act 2009.
- VPM Court Proceeding - Section 14 covers off procedures for Victoria Police.
	- An **informant** can make an application.
		- The informant serves a copy of the application on the accused.
		- The informant must forward the original brief to the Prosecutor, including the application for re-hearing, a new LEAP charge sequence, a revised list of unsuitable/suitable court dates and any material to be relied upon for the re-hearing application.
		- The informant *does not* need to attend on the day of the application.
	- An **accused** can make an application.
- Where a court *grants* a re-hearing application and set aside the original conviction:
	- The prosecutor must advise the informant of the date of the re-hearing; and
	- The informant must attend the re-hearing and ensure all the witnesses attend as if it is a contested hearing.
