# Purpose
The Sex Offenders Registration Act provides it's purpose as Section 1, which is summarised as:

> Require sexual offenders to keep police informed of their whereabouts and other personal details for a period of time.

And the above is done for the following reasons:
- to reduce the likelihood that they will re-offender.
- to facilitate the investigation and prosecuiton of any offences they commit.
- to prevent RSO's from working in child-related employment.

## Class 3 & 4 Offences
Class 3 offences are *discretionary*
- Person who is a serious sexual offender; penetrative offences against an adult. 

Class 4 offences are *discretionary*
- Person who is a serious sexual offender; Person who commits a sexual offences against an adult (non-penetrative offences against an adult).
