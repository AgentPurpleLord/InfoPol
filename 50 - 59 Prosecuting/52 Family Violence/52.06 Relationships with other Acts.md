# Magistrates' Court Rules 2018
## Regulation 4.08 Withdrawal of Application
An application under the Act may be withdrawn only with leave of the Court.
- Party seeking to withdraw an application must—
- File written notice of withdrawal to be served on registrar; or
- if person attend court, make oral application to court.

## Regulation 6.02 Striking out proceeding
Court may order that whole or part of app be struck out or amended if proceeding—
- Scandalous, frivolous or vexatious; or
- may prejudice, embarrass or delay fair hearing; or
- otherwise an abuse of the process of Court. 

## Regulation 15.01 Risk Assessment
A risk assessment is confidential and must not be—
1. used in evidence in any proceeding; or
2. disclosed to the RESP/associate of RESP to the proceeding with the risk assessment relates.
