Family violence intervention order court typically runs in the following format:
1. Police *initial action* (date of FV) -> compile *application for IVO* or *FVSN*.
2. Matter comes before court and:
	1. Negotiated by *FVCLO's*; or
	2. *Magistrate* makes decision.

# Application Methods
The following [[52.02 Application Methods|application methods]] are available:
- *App & Summons* -> [[52.04 Interim FV Orders|Interim Order]] or [[52.05 Final FV Orders|Final Order]] (*typically interim at first mention w/o AFM instruction*)
- *App & Warrant* -> [[52.04 Interim FV Orders|Interim Order]] or [[52.05 Final FV Orders|Final Order]]
- *[[52.03 FVSN|FVSN]]* -> [[52.04 Interim FV Orders|Interim Order]] or [[52.05 Final FV Orders|Final Order]]
 
The following are the [[52.02 Application Methods|application methods]] are available:
App & Summons -> [[52.04 Interim FV Orders|Interim IVO]] -> [[52.05 Final FV Orders|Final IVO]]
App & Warrant -> [[52.04 Interim FV Orders|Interim IVO]] -> [[52.05 Final FV Orders|Final IVO]]
[[52.03 FVSN|FVSN]] -> [[52.04 Interim FV Orders|Interim IVO]] -> [[52.05 Final FV Orders|Final IVO]]
