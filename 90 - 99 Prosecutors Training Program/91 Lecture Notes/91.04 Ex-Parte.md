- **Can only be used for summary offences**
- Magistrate has four options when non-appearance of accused charged with summary offence on summons:
	- If summons via ordinary service, direct accused be served personally; or
	- issue a warrant of arrest; or
	- proceed to hear and determine the charge in absence of accused; or
	- adjourn the proceeding on any terms considered appropriate
- If on bail for a summary offence:
	- Proceed to hear and determine the charge in the absence of the accused; or
	- adjourn the proceeding on any terms considered appropriate
- Fines of approx. $3,000 allowed for any charge, multiple charges can be up to $5,000
- Restitution cannot exceed $2,000
- Imprisonment cannot be available.
- A sentence requiring that consent of the accused, e.g. diversions, CCO's or adjourned undertakings is not allowed.
- Too serious to be heard via ex-parte
	- Family violence
	- Multiple priors for similar offending
	- Victim who may want to make a VIS
- Consider what is the likely penalty if the matter is finalised?

- When consideration **bench warrant**:
	- Is the charge summary offence?
	- Sufficient evidence on the brief?
	- Sentence likely to be a modest fine?
	- Matter suitable to be determined ex-parte, having regard to seriousness of offence and criminal record?
