- Magistrate will ask to step through legislation; keep copy of Bail Act ready.

# Moot Stages

## Prior to Hearing
- Witness / Evidence in Chief; as in speak to informant prior to hearing and ensure:
	- Do they copy of **statement**?
	- Given **evidence** before?
	- Do they know **oath** or **affirmation**?
	- Any further forensic analysis of exhibits / **further evidence** not included on remand app.
	- Any **delay** re obtaining further evidence, e.g. statements, CCTV, phone download, drug analysis?
- Defence; as in speak to defence and ensure:
	- Defence have **brief**?
	- Bail **threshold** agreed?
	- Any proposed **addresses**? Informant to make checks. 

## Matter is called
- **APPEARANCE:** "Senior Constable Nathan HAYMES appearing for the prosecution your honour."
- **POSITION:** "Your Honour, Police are opposing bail."
- **THRESHOLD:**
	- Take HH through the Bail Act, literally referring to the section numbers and wording.
	- Who bears burden of establishing threshold? What section covers this?
	- Unacceptable risks? If risks are founded, bail **MUST** be refused.

## Call the informant
- Oath / Affirmation? **Who actually asks this? Is it pros or is it HH?**
- Adduce evidence-in-chief:
	- Open questions. Who? What? Where? When? How?
	- Cover any surrounding circumstances if possible (s. 3AAA)
	- Cover any unacceptable risks if possible
- **CLARIFICATION:** "I'll take you back to...." OR "You said earlier that..." OR "When was that? Where was that? Who did that? Who seized that? Was anyone else there?"
- **EXHIBITS:** Show, Identify, Verify, TENDER.
	- PROS: "May I approach the witness your Honour?" -> Show Defence before showing witness.
	- PROS: "I am going to show you a document." -> hand it to the witness.
	- PROS: "What have I handed you?" OR "Describe what I've just handed you."
	- WITNESS: "That's a photograph of X."
	- PROS: "Who took that photograph?" 
	- WITNESS: "I did."
	- PROS: "Your Honour, I tender that photograph."
	- The same process occurs with criminal priors. **do we tender these via the informant?**
- **END:** "That's the **evidence-in-chief** from this witness your honour."

## Re-Examination
- Take notes during the Cross Examination.
- Clarify anything that was made muddy.
- **NO RE-EXAM:** "That is the evidence of this witness your honour, may the witness be excused?"

## Cross Examination of Defence Witnesses
**KEY RULE:** Make a factual statement with a *question* attached.

- "You're a busy person, aren't you?"
- "For 8 hours of the day you won't be home, will you?"
- "You didn't know about these prior offences, did you?"
- "That's because he keeps secrets from you, isn't that right?"

Example of script:
- Q: You didn't know about these prior offences, did you?
- A: Yeah, I knew that he used drugs.
- Q: But you didn't contact police, did you? And the reason you didn't contact police is that you didn't want him to get into trouble, isn't that right?

Don't say: "How are you going to supervise him?"; do say: "You can't supervise him all the time, can you?"

## Final Submissions
- Has the threshold been made out by defence?
- Look at 3AAA, address as many sub-sections as possible.
- Address unacceptable risks and the test.

## Post-Hearing
- New Court Date
- Decision made; bail conditions if any, etc.
- BMS coversheet update? Record notes in daybook etc.
- Notify informant and have informant notify victim.
- Notify informant to prepare preliminary brief etc .
