Failing to disclosure information can cause:
- Damage to reputation; and
- Improper convictions; and
- Costs; and
- Appeals; and
- Effect the communities confidence.

# Criminal Procedure Act
- s. 39(1A) - when a full brief must be served
	- (a) when a prelim brief served within 21 days after day of charge-sheet filed, at any time after summary case conference is held; or
	- (b) in any other case, at any time after criminal proceeding has commenced.
- s. 39(2) - if the accused gives notice under 39(1) in writing, informant must serve on accused at least 14 days before:
	- (a) CME; or
	- (b) if no CME, summary hearing.
- s. 39(3) Magistrates' Court has power to make order earlier or later than the date for service required by subsection (2).
	- **Important, this can happen in mention court, you must notify the informant.**
- Contents of a full brief
	- s. 41(1)(e) - any other information, document or thing in possession of the prosecution that is relevant to the alleged offence including...
	- (iii) a copy of every document relevant to the alleged offence that the prosecution does not intend to rely on.
	- s. 42 must disclose on-going
- *Victim Impact Statements (VIS)* are disclosable. Only obtain these **AFTER** plea of guilty.
- Section 48 - must not disclose details of address, telephone number of any person in any information, document or thing; unless
	- details do not identify particular person; or
	- address / telephone number relevant to offence charged; or
	- disclosure not likely to present risk to welfare/physical safety of any person; or
	- Magistrates' Court permits disclosure in accordance with s48(3).
- Disclosure of improper conduct by public officer is material covered by Protected Disclosures Act 2012. Should not be disclosed.
- Cannot disclose that person is in witness protection, nor identity or location, including people who were or are currently in witness protection.
- s. 41A - summary stream
	- Disclosure certificate must be served with full brief and filed with the court within 7 days of service.
- s. 110A - indictable stream
	- Disclosure certificate served with hand-up brief and filed with court/DPP within 7 days of service.
- s. 416A non-disclosure orders
