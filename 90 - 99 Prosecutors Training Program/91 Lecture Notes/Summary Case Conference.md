- s. 54(2) provides that a SCC **must** occur before a matter can go to a contest mention, summary hearing or before a request of a full brief is made.
- If the accused is unrepresented, Magistrates' Court may dispense with the requirement for a SCC (s. 54(5)).
- s. 54(7) anything said or done in the course of SCC; or any document prepared solely for the purpose of SCC is not admissible in any proceeding before any court.
	- Unless; parties to SCC agree to giving that evidence; or proceeding is criminal offence in connection with the SCC.

# Conducting SCC
- Check the brief - determine prospect of successful prosecution.
- Identify issues in dispute; can they be resolved through further disclosure and appropriate negotiation.
- Identify lesser charges.
- Liaise with informants re: withdrawal/further evidence/amendments to summaries.
