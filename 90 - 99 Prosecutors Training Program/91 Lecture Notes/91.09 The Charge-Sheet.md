- First thing you should do is check the charges of a brief.
- Commencement of criminal proceedings
	- s. 5(a) CPA 2009 - filing or signing of charge-sheet via s.6
	- s. 6(1) CPA 2009 - filing charge with MC; or filing charge with BJ; or at time of charge signed by informant per s. 14
- Time limits for filing a charge (statute of limitations)
	- Proceedings commence when s14 process is utilised or filing of a charge sheet.
	- Where an accused is adult:
		- Summary offences = 12 months
		- indictable offences = any time
		- drug matters (summary) = 3 yrs
		- disturbing religious worship = 3 months
	- Where accused is a child:
		- Summary = 6 months
			- Can use s. 344B or 344C of CYFA 2005 to app to extend to 12 mth
		- Indictable = any time.
- The dates on the charges
	- The date of the summons must be either the same date as the charge, or the charge date must be earlier than the summons date.
- Summons issued by informant s 14 procedure
	- Charge sheet and summons must be filed with the registrar within 7 days of signing the charge-sheet
		- If it isn't within 7 days, costs against CCP.
- Re amending charges;
	- Sections 8 or 9 has to do with date or location.
	- Schedule 1 has to do with description of property or something similar.
