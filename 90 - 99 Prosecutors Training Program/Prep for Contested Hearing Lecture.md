# Prep Notes
- Preliminaries
	- s. 127 Mag Court Act - Witnesses Order
	- Amend Charges
	- Agreed Facts (s. 191 EA)
- Charges
- Witnesses
	- In the order that you would call them.
- Exhibits
	- In the order you would tender/show them.
- Summary; short SOAF style summary of what happened.
- Submissions
- Pre-court conferencing
	- 5 minutes with defence
		- Amendments to charges
		- Agreed facts + sign them
		- Identify any rules of evidence issues
	- 5 minutes with informant
	- Are you going to make oath/affirmation?
- EIC + XE + REX:
	- State your name, rank and work location.
	- State your name and occupation.
	- That is the evidence-in-chief of this witness.
	- *After XE / REX* That is the evidence of this witness, may the witness be excused.

# Final Submissions
- Elements of the offence are ...
- YH we say element 1 can be proven by consent...
- We say that element 2 can be proven by evidence you heard from ...
- Anticipate as much as you can for what defence are going to say
- Consider seeking leave to refer to facts of the case; no harm in seeking permission.
	- "Your Honour, I am going to make final submission. I will be referring to facts of the case. I seek leave to do so as part of my final submissions."
