# Issue
AFM has been excused by Magistrate under s. 18 of Evidence Act (spouse evidence).
Call second witness (police who took statement); objection as hearsay evidence?

# Rule
s. 59 Evidence Act 2008
Dictionary - Unavailability, representation, previous representation (1)(g)
s. 62 - First-hand Hearsay
s. 65 - Maker Unavailable (2)(b)
s. 67 - Notice

# Application
s. 59: In this case, it is conceded that the evidence given by the police officer is hearsay.

s. 62: In this case, the victim provided her representation of the asserted facts, which came from her personal knowledge. A statement was provided to police which detailed what she saw, heard and experienced. It is therefore considered to be first-hand hearsay. 

s. 65(1): In this case, the victim is an unavailable witness. 

Dictionary - Unavailability: The victim falls within (1)(g), as she has made a successful application pursuant to s. 18 of the Evidence Act. Therefore all reasonable steps have been taken to compel this person to give evidence, but they have not been successful.

s. 65(2)(b): As the victim is unavailable. The victim's previous representation was made shortly after the asserted facts that occurred. Additionally, the victim's previous representation was provided in a sworn statement to police, meaning it is unlikely to be a fabrications. It is for these reasons an exception to the hearsay rule applies.
