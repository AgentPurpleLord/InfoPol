- Witness, police member, has attended a collision scene.
- Giving evidence, vehicle was not in a safe condition.
- Defence object on basis that it is opinion evidence.
- "I attended the collision scene, I determined that the accused was on the wrong side of the road doing 150kph"


It is opinion evidence; s. 76; it is proving existence of a fact.
Exception; s. 79; specialised knowledge; ask witness about training, study or experience; its not hearsay; based on something they saw/heard/perceived; 
Have them show their 'working out' how did they make that conclusion?
- They give evidence, HWP, MCIU, investigated 90 crashes, they were reconstructionist on 5 collisions, now have a qualitifcation as a reconstructionist.

Talk about opinion being based from their training/experience, e.g. wholly/substantially based on that knowledge.
