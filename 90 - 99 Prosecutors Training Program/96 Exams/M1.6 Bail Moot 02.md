# Trafficking
1. The accused intentionally *trafficked* or *attempted to traffick* in a particular substance; and
2. That it was a *drug of dependence* that the accused intentionally trafficked or attempted to traffick.

Section 70(1) of the Drugs Act includes definition as "selling, exchanging, agreeing to sell, offering for sale or having in possession for sale, a drug of dependence."
