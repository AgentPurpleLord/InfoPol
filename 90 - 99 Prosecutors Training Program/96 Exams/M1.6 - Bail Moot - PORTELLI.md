### Traffick Methylamphetamine
Found at *Charge 6* in PORTELLI brief.
1. "The accused at Frankston on today did traffick a drug of dependence namely methylamphetamine."
2. ~ **Schedule 2 Item 24(b):** An offence against any of the following provisions of the Drugs, Poisons and Controlled Substances Act 1981- (b) section 71AC (trafficking in drug of dependence).
3. ~ **Section 71AC DPCSA Trafficking:** 
	1. ~ (1) Subject to (2), a person who, without being authorised by or licensed under this Act or the regulations or the Voluntary Assisted Dying Act 2017 or the regulations under the Act to do so, trafficks or attempts to traffick in a drug of dependence is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to *level 4 imprisonment (15 years maximum).*
	2. ~ (2) A person who, without being authorised by or licensed under this Act or the regulations or the Voluntary Assisted Dying Act 2017, or the regulations under that Act to do so, trafficks or attempts to traffick in a drug of dependence at a school or in a public place within 500 metres of a school is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to *level 3 imprisonment (20 years maximum).*
	3. (i) That the accused *intentionally "trafficked"* or "attempted to traffick" in a particular substance; and
	4. (ii) That it was a *"drug of dependence"* that the accused intentionally trafficked or attempted to traffick.
4. ~ **Section 70 DPCSA Definitions:** 
	1. ~ *traffick* in relation to a drug of dependence includes (a) prepare a drug of dependednce for trafficking; or (b) manufacture a drug of dependence; or *(c) sell, exchange, agree to sell, offer for sale or have in possession for sale, a drug of dependence.*
	3. From Charge Book: This definition is *inclusive* meaning that it is also possible to rely upon the common law definition.
5. ~ **Possessing a Drug of Dependence for Sale**
	1. The prosecution must prove: 
		1. the accused possessed a drug of dependence; and
		2. the accused intended to sell that drug.
	2. *Possession* is *not* in dispute; the drugs were found on the accused.
	3. Prosecution only needs to prove "that the accused had a *general intention to sell* the drug in the future. It is not necessary to prove that a particular sale was in contemplation at the material time, or that the accused had a specific buyer in mind."
6. ~ **Schedule 11 DPCSA Weights for Methylamphetamine:**
	1. ~ Large Commercial Quantity = 500g
	2. ~ Large Commercial Quantity in mixture = 700g
	3. ~ Commercial Quantity = 50g
	4. ~ Commercial Quantity in mixture = 250g
	5. ~ Automatic forfeiture quantity = 30g
	6. ~ *Traffickable Quantity = 3g*
	7. ~ Small Quantity = 0.75g
