# Question 2
An accused is charged with stalking the complainant and is currently on an adjourned undertaking for unlawful assault against the same victim (former partner). What is the bail position?

- Stalking -> Schedule 2 -> Item 8
	- Must be that accused within the preceding 10 years, been convicted or found guilty of an offence against that section, which acc. used or threatened violence. 
- Outstanding:
	- Adjourned Undertaking:
		- Unlawful Assault against same victim.

Applicable Test: s. 4AA(3)
Section re: Applicable Test: s. 4C(1)
Schedule and item number which causes test: Schedule 2 Item 8(a/b), in that accused has a previous conviction within 10 years in relation to an offence where the accused used violence (unlawful assault)
Section re: Unacceptable Risk Test: s. 4D(a)
Section discusses Unacceptable Risk Test: s. 4E(1)(a)(iaa) and (i) and (iii), as in the accused might commit further stalking offences against the victim, therefore being Sch. 2 Item 8 offences and previous assault shows accused has propensity to assault victim and interfere with witness, victim might be hesitant to give evidence in matter if stalking continues.

- Stalking

	- Extensive fail to appear on bail history.

Schedule and item number which causes test:
- Intentionally Cause Serious Injury = Schedule 2, Item 6
- Threats to Kill = Schedule 2, Item 7 - family violence offending.

Section discusses Unacceptable Risk Test: s. 4E(1)(iaa) & (i) & (iii) & (iv), further stalking charges would be Sch. 2, clearly endangered the safety of the AFM re: injuries, possibility of interfering with the victim in the matter and extensive FTAB history all go towards unacceptable risk.
