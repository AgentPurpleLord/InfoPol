# Role of the Prosecution
- To tell the court what happened, i.e. when someone who is at a bail application, why does he need to be locked up?
- To tell a story or be a storyteller
- You tell a story through others, such as witnesses.
- Say less, tell a clear story with simple wording.

# Evidence-in-Chief
- Try not to assume facts
- Ask questions in a chronological way.
- Ask because you want to know what the answer is.

# Case Conepts or Witness Theories
- You should form a theory about what each witness provides the case.
- Lots of different kinds of witnesses, which one is yours?
	- Are they reliable? Do they add XYZ to your case?
- If you're cross-examining them, how do **you** want to paint this witness out to be to the court?
