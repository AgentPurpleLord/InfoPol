**Advocacy** is the *art of persuasion*. You should be confident, but yourself. Project your own personality, don't get tied down in lingo and don't use big words that aren't in your usual vocabulary.
**If you lack credibility, you lack persuasion.**

# Defence Advocate
- Defend their client as best as possible.
- Act with the best interest of their client.
- Defence cannot conduct a defence knowing it to be false.
- Even if they lose your respect, treat them with courtesy, as that's your obligation as a prosecutor.
