## Regarding "pig"
**OPEN**: You saw the brawl occurring that night, correct?
**OPEN**: And you saw uniformed police officers, correct?
**OPEN**: And you observed the marked divisional van, correct?
**OPEN**: So you knew that police officers were in the area, performing arrests that night, didn't you?
**OPEN**: And you saw a person in normal attire assisting the police, correct?
**OPEN**: You know pig is common derogatory term for police officers, don't you?
**GATE SHUT**: You assumed that the person in normal attire was a police officer, didn't you?
**GATE SHUT:** You knew he was trying to arrest your friend and that he was a police officer, and you didn't want that to happen, did you?
**GATE SHUT**: You called the officer a pig because you knew he was an officer, didn't you?
