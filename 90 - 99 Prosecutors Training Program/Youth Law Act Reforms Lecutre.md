- Obligation under legislaiton, prosecution must review all briefs:
	- Evidence to rebut doli incapax.
	- Evidence to est. each element brd.
	- reasonable prospect of conviction.
- Timeframe is ASAP, no later than 21 days if practicable.
- This review must be provided to the child or legal rep.
