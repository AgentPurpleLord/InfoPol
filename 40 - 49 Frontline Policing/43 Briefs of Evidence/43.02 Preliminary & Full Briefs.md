>[!INFO] What is a preliminary brief?
>A series of documents utilised by police prosecutors to provide an overview of the Prosecution (or 'Crown') case. A preliminary brief is compiled by the [[Informant]] of an investigaiton.

A Preliminary or Full Brief of Evidence is a series of documents utilised by police to provide an overview of the prosecution (or Crown) case. It sets out a 'case concept' or how exactly the prosecution will prove the elements of an offence (see [[20 Offences]] points of proof for each offence).

>[!WARNING]- Before End of Shift Responsibilities
>1. Submit LEDR MK2 (crime reports)
>2. Submit AIM Package to SGT for review
>3. Submit Brief Head Form to Admin with Interview Discs (if offender processed)

>[!WARNING] Brief of Evidence Timeframes
>1. Brief **must** be compiled within 30 days of interview (submitted for 1st checking).
>2. Brief amendments **must** be completed within 7 days of being returned.
